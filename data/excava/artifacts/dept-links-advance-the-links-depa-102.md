**Decision:** Advance the links department's mission by auditing and updating the Mission Control Dashboard's links.
**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool on the Mission Control Dashboard's live links to produce a report of broken, redirected, or slow-loading links.
2. Verify the report includes actionable recommendations for updating or removing problematic links.
3. Review and prioritize the report's findings based on severity and impact.
4. Update the link inventory with fixes for broken, redirected, or slow-loading links.
5. Publish the verified, updated link inventory for the Mission Control Dashboard.
6. Monitor and review the updated links for ongoing performance and accuracy.
**What changed:** The links department will now have an updated and verified link inventory with actionable recommendations for maintenance and improvement.
