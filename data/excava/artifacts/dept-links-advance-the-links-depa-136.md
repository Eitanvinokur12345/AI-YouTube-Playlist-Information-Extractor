# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-136` (dept) · 2026-08-01T04:04:14.124646+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the department’s current link inventory to generate a report of broken, redirected, or outdated links.
2. Anchor compiles and shares a verified list of active links alongside broken/redirected ones, including reasons and fixes, in a single shared document.
3. The team reviews the document and prioritizes fixes based on urgency and impact.
4. Assign owners to address each broken/redirected link with the provided fixes.
5. Verify resolved links and update the document with status changes.
6. Archive the final report for future reference and team alignment.

**What changed:** A structured, actionable link inventory report with clear ownership and next steps.
