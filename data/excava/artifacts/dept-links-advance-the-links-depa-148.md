# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-148` (dept) · 2026-07-31T05:04:59.439977+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Audit the Mission Control Dashboard’s link inventory via the department’s spreadsheet to generate a live snapshot of all tracked links.
2. Curate the inventory by verifying status, source, and last update for each link.
3. Deliver a cleaned inventory with updated status, source, and last update to the department’s spreadsheet.
4. Flag any broken, outdated, or irrelevant links for review or removal.
5. Prioritize updates based on mission-critical links or recent changes.
6. Document any gaps or inconsistencies in the inventory for follow-up.

**What changed:** A structured audit and curation process for the Mission Control Dashboard’s link inventory.
