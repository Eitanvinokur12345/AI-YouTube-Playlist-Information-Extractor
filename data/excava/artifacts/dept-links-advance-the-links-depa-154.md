**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory.
2. Anchor delivers a prioritized report of broken/outdated links with their locations.
3. Team reviews the report to assess impact and urgency.
4. Assign fixes to relevant team members based on link location.
5. Implement corrections and verify fixes via the link-checker.
6. Document resolved links and update the department’s link inventory.
