# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-156` (dept) · 2026-07-31T21:29:09.767580+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to generate a fresh report.
2. Anchor compiles the results into a prioritized list of broken/outdated links.
3. Anchor publishes the prioritized real-time link audit report by EOD.
4. Team reviews the report and assigns cleanup tasks by the next business day.
5. Team addresses broken links in order of priority within 48 hours of report publication.
6. Anchor verifies fixes and updates the report with resolution status.

**What changed:** A structured, time-bound process for link auditing and cleanup was formalized.
