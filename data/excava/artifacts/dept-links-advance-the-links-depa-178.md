# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-178` (dept) · 2026-07-30T19:10:46.522856+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the Mission Control Dashboard’s live inventory of links.
2. Anchor compiles a prioritized report of broken/outdated external links from the tool’s output.
3. LinLea reviews and validates the report for accuracy and relevance to the department’s mission.
4. Anchor distributes the finalized report to the team for immediate review and action.
5. Team members address broken/outdated links based on the prioritized report.
6. LinLea tracks progress and updates the Mission Control Dashboard to reflect resolved links.

**What changed:** External links in the Mission Control Dashboard will be systematically audited and corrected.
