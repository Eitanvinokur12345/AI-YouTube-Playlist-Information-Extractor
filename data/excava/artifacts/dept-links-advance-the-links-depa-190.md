# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-190` (dept) · 2026-07-30T18:22:45.660410+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the Mission Control Dashboard’s live inventory of external links.
2. Anchor generates a clean report identifying broken, duplicate, or low-value links.
3. Anchor prioritizes the report and submits it to the team for removal or replacement.
4. The team reviews and approves the prioritized report.
5. The team executes link removals/replacements based on the approved report.
6. Anchor verifies the updates and confirms resolution of identified issues.

**What changed:** External links in the Mission Control Dashboard will be audited, prioritized, and cleaned for redundancy and dead ends.
