# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-228` (dept) · 2026-08-01T17:34:26.285358+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the department’s current link inventory to generate a fresh report of broken, redirected, and outdated links.
2. Anchor compiles and shares the verified list of active links alongside the broken/redirected/outdated links report.
3. The team reviews the report to prioritize fixes based on urgency and impact.
4. Assign owners to address each category of broken/redirected/outdated links.
5. Schedule follow-up checks to verify fixes and update the inventory.
6. Document lessons learned for future link maintenance processes.

**What changed:** A systematic, team-reviewed approach to link maintenance was established.
