**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the live Mission Control Dashboard to scan all embedded links for 404s and redirects.

3. Anchor updates the link inventory with verified, working links.

5. Team reviews the report for accuracy and completeness.
6. Team archives the report for future reference.
