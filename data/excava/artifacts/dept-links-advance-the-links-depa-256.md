**Decision:** Advance the links department's mission by verifying and updating links in the Mission Control Dashboard.
**Plan:**
1. Run a link-checker on the Mission Control Dashboard’s inventory file to identify broken or outdated links.
2. Export a report of all broken or redirected links to produce a clean list of dead links and their replacements.
3. Verify each link in the report to confirm the issues and identify necessary fixes.
4. Update the broken or outdated links with their replacements or corrections.
5. Publish the finalized, dead-link-free Mission Control Dashboard with verified links.
6. Include a summary of fixes in the published dashboard for transparency and accountability.
**What changed:** The links department will have a verified and updated link inventory, ensuring the Mission Control Dashboard's integrity and reliability.
