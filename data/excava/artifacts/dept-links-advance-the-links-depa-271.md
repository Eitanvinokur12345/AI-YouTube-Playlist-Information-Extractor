**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the Mission Control Dashboard’s live inventory.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report of all broken/outdated external links.
3. LinLea reviews the report and flags high-priority links for immediate action.
4. The links team addresses broken links first, then updates outdated ones.
5. Anchor verifies fixes and re-runs the tool to confirm resolution.
6. LinLea documents the final report and shares it with the department.
