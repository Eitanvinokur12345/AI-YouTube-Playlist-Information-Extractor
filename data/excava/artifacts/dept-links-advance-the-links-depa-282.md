**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor (links-w1) runs a Screaming Frog crawl of the department’s link inventory, filtering for 404s, 301 chains, and last-modified dates older than 12 months.
2. Anchor compiles the crawl results into a spreadsheet, ranking decayed/low-value links by traffic loss and last activity.
3. Anchor delivers the prioritized list to LinLea by EOD today.
4. LinLea reviews the list for alignment with department goals.

6. Anchor documents the changes in a tracking sheet for future audits.
