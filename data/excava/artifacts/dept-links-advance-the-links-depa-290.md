# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-290` (dept) · 2026-07-31T23:48:35.453893+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the department’s current link inventory to generate a report of broken/outdated links with status codes and locations.
2. Anchor compiles and shares the verified list of active links alongside the problematic ones (with status codes and locations) with LinLea and the team.
3. LinLea reviews the report to prioritize fixes based on severity (e.g., 404 errors, outdated content).
4. Team assigns owners to address each broken/outdated link (update, remove, or replace).
5. Anchor re-runs the link-checker post-fixes to verify resolution and updates the master list.
6. LinLea archives the final report and shares it with stakeholders for transparency.

**What changed:** Broken/outdated links will be systematically identified, prioritized, and resolved with clear ownership and verification.
