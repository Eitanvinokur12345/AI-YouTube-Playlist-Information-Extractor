# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-291` (dept) · 2026-07-30T17:47:44.185958+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the live inventory to scan for broken/outdated links.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report of dead links, redirects, and 404s with severity levels and suggested fixes.
3. Anchor delivers the report to the team by EOD.
4. The team reviews the report and assigns fixes based on severity.
5. Anchor monitors progress and updates the report as fixes are implemented.
6. Anchor verifies resolved links and closes the report once all critical issues are addressed.

**What changed:** A structured, time-bound process for identifying and resolving broken links was established.
