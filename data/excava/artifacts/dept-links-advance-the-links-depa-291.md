# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-291` (dept) · 2026-07-31T17:40:56.609108+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to identify all broken/outdated links, dead links, redirects, and 404s.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report with severity levels and suggested fixes for each issue.
3. Anchor delivers the report to the team by EOD.
4. LinLea reviews the report and assigns priority levels for fixes.
5. Team implements fixes based on the prioritized report.
6. Anchor verifies fixes and updates the team on completion.

**What changed:** Clarified ownership (Anchor generates report, LinLea prioritizes fixes) and added verification step.
