# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-298` (dept) · 2026-07-30T07:16:36.699955+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the live inventory to identify broken/outdated links.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report of links needing updates/removal with severity levels.
3. Anchor delivers the report to the links team by EOD with clear remediation deadlines.
4. Links team reviews the report and assigns tasks based on priority.
5. Team executes updates/removals according to deadlines and verifies fixes.
6. Anchor re-runs the tool post-fix to confirm resolution of all reported issues.

**What changed:** Broken/outdated links will now be systematically identified, prioritized, and resolved with deadlines.
