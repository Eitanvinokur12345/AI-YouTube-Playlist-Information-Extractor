# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-324` (dept) · 2026-07-27T22:18:38.302209+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs a fresh crawl of the department’s link inventory using Screaming Frog to generate a complete dataset of internal/external links, status codes, and anchor text.
2. Anchor exports the raw crawl data and filters it to identify broken links.
3. Anchor delivers a clean, prioritized list of broken links to the lead for review.
4. The lead analyzes the list and assigns remediation tasks (e.g., fixing, redirecting, or removing broken links).
5. Anchor tracks progress on link fixes and updates the inventory quarterly.

**What changed:** Fresh crawl data replaces outdated link inventory for accurate remediation.
