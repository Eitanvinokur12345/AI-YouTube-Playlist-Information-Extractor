**Decision:** Advance the links department's mission by auditing and cleaning the link inventory.
**Plan:**
1. Run a full audit of the department’s link inventory using the link-checker tool.
2. Generate a complete report of all active, broken, and redirected links across our systems.
3. Deliver the report to identify areas for improvement.
4. Remove dead links from the inventory based on the audit report.
5. Update the link inventory with the cleaned data.
**What changed:** The links department will have an up-to-date and cleaned link inventory.
