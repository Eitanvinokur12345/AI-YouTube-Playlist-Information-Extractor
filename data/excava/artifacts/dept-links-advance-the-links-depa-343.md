# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-343` (dept) · 2026-08-03T18:38:24.146669+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, Tether, LinLea · synthesized by groq/llama-3.3-70b-versatile

**Decision:** Advance the links department's mission by auditing and updating existing links on the Mission Control Dashboard.
**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool on the Mission Control Dashboard’s live links to identify broken or redirected links.
2. Generate a report listing all broken or redirected links for review and verification.
3. Verify and update the links based on the report to ensure accuracy and reliability.
4. Compile a verified, updated link inventory for the Mission Control Dashboard.
5. Publish the updated link inventory for access and reference.
**What changed:** The link inventory for the Mission Control Dashboard will be audited, updated, and published to ensure reliability and accuracy.
