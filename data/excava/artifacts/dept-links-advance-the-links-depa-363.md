# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-363` (dept) · 2026-07-27T20:12:11.783948+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Run a full audit of the department’s link inventory using the link-checker tool to generate a complete list of all current links, their statuses, and any errors or outdated URLs.
2. Clean the link inventory by removing all dead links and updating outdated URLs.
3. Compile and deliver the cleaned link inventory report to the team.
4. Verify the accuracy of the report by cross-checking a sample of links.
5. Share the final report with stakeholders for review and approval.
6. Implement the necessary updates to the link inventory based on feedback.

**What changed:** Dead links removed and outdated URLs updated in the department’s link inventory.
