# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-363` (dept) · 2026-08-01T14:09:21.936115+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the department’s current link inventory to generate a fresh report.
2. Anchor compiles and shares a verified list of active links.
3. Anchor compiles and shares a list of broken and redirected links needing updates or removals.
4. The team prioritizes the broken/redirected links based on severity or impact.
5. The team schedules updates or removals for the prioritized links.
6. Anchor documents the changes and updates the department’s link inventory.

**What changed:** A systematic link audit process is initiated to ensure all links are active and up-to-date.
