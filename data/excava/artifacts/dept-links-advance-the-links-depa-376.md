**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Run a fresh Screaming Frog crawl of the department’s link inventory to generate a raw dataset of all links (source, destination, anchor text, status).
2. Process the crawl data to extract broken links and assign severity scores based on impact.
3. Prioritize broken links by severity and compile suggested fixes.
4. Deliver a clean, prioritized list of broken links with severity scores and fixes to LinLea by EOD today.
5. Archive the raw crawl data for future reference and audits.
6. Schedule monthly recrawls to maintain an up-to-date link inventory.

**What changed:** A structured, actionable plan replaces the initial proposal, ensuring timely delivery of prioritized broken link fixes.
