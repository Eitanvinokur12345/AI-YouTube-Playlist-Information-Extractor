# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-416` (dept) · 2026-07-31T22:36:45.294199+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the department’s current link inventory to identify broken or outdated links.
2. Anchor compiles the broken link audit report, listing all invalid links, their locations, and status codes.
3. Anchor shares the report with the links team for review and prioritization.
4. The links team assigns owners to address each invalid link based on severity and impact.
5. Owners update or remove the invalid links, verifying fixes with the link-checker tool.
6. Anchor re-runs the tool to confirm all links are valid and updates the team on completion.

**What changed:** A structured audit and remediation process for broken links is now formalized.
