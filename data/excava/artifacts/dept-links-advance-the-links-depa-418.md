# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-418` (dept) · 2026-08-01T10:19:38.485575+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the department’s current link inventory to generate a fresh report.
2. Anchor compiles and shares a verified list of active links and a prioritized report of broken links needing updates or removals.
3. The department reviews the broken links report and assigns owners to address each issue.
4. Owners update or remove broken links based on the prioritized list.
5. Anchor re-runs the link-checker to verify fixes and confirms all links are active.
6. Anchor documents the final link inventory and shares it with the team.

**What changed:** A systematic process for auditing, prioritizing, and fixing broken links was established.
