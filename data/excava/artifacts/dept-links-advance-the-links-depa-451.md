# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-451` (dept) · 2026-07-30T03:44:14.633772+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to identify broken or outdated links.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report of problematic links with severity levels (e.g., critical, high, medium).
3. Anchor shares the report with the team for immediate review.
4. The team addresses critical links first, followed by high and medium priority links.
5. Anchor updates the inventory with fixed links and verifies corrections.
6. Anchor documents the process and outcomes for future reference.

**What changed:** Broken/outdated links will be systematically identified, prioritized, and resolved.
