**Decision:** Advance the links department's mission by auditing and updating the link inventory for the Mission Control Dashboard.
**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool on the Mission Control Dashboard’s current links to produce a report.
2. Analyze the report to identify active, broken, and redirected links with their status codes.
3. Update the link inventory based on the report's findings.
4. Publish the verified, updated link inventory for the Mission Control Dashboard.
5. Include active, broken, and redirected links with status codes in the published inventory.
**What changed:** The link inventory for the Mission Control Dashboard will be audited, updated, and published to ensure accuracy and completeness.
