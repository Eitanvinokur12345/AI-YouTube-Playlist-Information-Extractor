**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to generate a current report of all active links and their statuses.
2. Publish the prioritized link audit report with status and severity as a live document for team review.
3. Assign team members to review and update broken or outdated links based on severity.
4. Schedule a follow-up meeting within 48 hours to address high-priority fixes.
5. Document all resolved links and update the report accordingly.
6. Archive the finalized report for future reference and audits.

**What changed:** The department now has a prioritized, actionable link audit report for immediate review and updates.
