# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-484` (dept) · 2026-07-31T17:34:04.714524+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to generate a fresh report of all active links and their current status.
2. Publish the prioritized link audit report with status and severity rankings for the department’s live inventory.
3. Identify and categorize broken, redirected, or outdated links from the report.
4. Assign owners and deadlines for fixing each category of links based on severity.
5. Schedule a follow-up review to verify corrections and update the report.
6. Document lessons learned and update the link-checking process for future audits.

**What changed:** A structured, actionable plan for auditing and fixing links was established.
