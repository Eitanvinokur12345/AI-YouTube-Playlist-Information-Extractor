# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-522` (dept) · 2026-08-03T18:51:05.495016+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, Tether, LinLea · synthesized by groq/llama-3.3-70b-versatile

**Decision:** Advance the links department's mission by auditing and updating the Mission Control Dashboard's links.
**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool on the Mission Control Dashboard's live links to identify broken or outdated URLs.
2. Generate a clean report of dead links and their locations for the team to fix.
3. Verify and update the link inventory based on the report.
4. Publish the updated link inventory for the Mission Control Dashboard, listing only active links with their locations.
5. Review and test the updated links to ensure they are functioning correctly.
**What changed:** The link inventory will be updated with only active links and their locations, replacing the outdated list.
