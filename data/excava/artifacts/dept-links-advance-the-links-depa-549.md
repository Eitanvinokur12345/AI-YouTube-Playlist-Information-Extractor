**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the department’s current link inventory to identify broken URLs.
2. Anchor compiles and shares the broken link audit report with the links team by EOD.
3. The links team reviews the report and prioritizes dead links for fixes.
4. Assign owners to each broken link for resolution.
5. Implement fixes for high-priority broken links within one week.
6. Verify all corrected links and update the link inventory.

**What changed:** Broken link audit process initiated with team accountability.
