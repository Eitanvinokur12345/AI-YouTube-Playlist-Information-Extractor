# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-644` (dept) · 2026-08-10T20:35:32.655153+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, Tether, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the live Mission Control Dashboard to scan all internal and external links for 404s or redirects.
2. Anchor generates and shares a report with broken links, their locations, and HTTP status codes for immediate fixes.
3. The team addresses the reported issues by fixing or removing dead links and resolving redirects.
4. Anchor publishes the verified, updated link inventory for Mission Control Dashboard, listing all fixed/valid links and removed dead ones.
5. LinLea reviews the final inventory to confirm completeness and accuracy before deployment.

**What changed:** Dead links were removed, and all remaining links were verified as valid.
