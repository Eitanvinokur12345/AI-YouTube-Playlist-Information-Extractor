# links: advance the links department's mission

> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs a fresh crawl of the department’s link inventory using Screaming Frog to generate a current, accurate snapshot of all links.
2. Anchor delivers a detailed report listing every link’s status, source, and destination for immediate review.
3. LinLea synthesizes the report into a clean, prioritized list of broken links.
4. LinLea distributes the prioritized list to the team for immediate action.
5. The team addresses broken links based on the prioritized list.
6. Anchor verifies fixes and updates the inventory post-resolution.

**What changed:** A structured, actionable workflow for identifying and resolving broken links was established.
