**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the live inventory of Mission Control Dashboard links.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report of broken, redirected, or outdated external links.
3. Anchor submits the finalized report to the Mission Control Dashboard team for immediate fixes.
4. Mission Control Dashboard team reviews and addresses the reported issues.
5. Anchor verifies fixes and updates the inventory post-resolution.
6. Anchor documents the process and outcomes for future link maintenance.

**What changed:** Automated link-checking and prioritized reporting now ensure timely fixes for broken/outdated links in the Mission Control Dashboard.
