# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-697` (dept) · 2026-07-30T19:32:15.639426+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the live inventory of Mission Control Dashboard links.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report of broken, redirected, or outdated external links.
3. Anchor shares the report with the team for review and action.
4. Team addresses high-priority broken links first, followed by redirects and outdated content.
5. Team updates or removes flagged links in the Mission Control Dashboard.
6. Anchor verifies fixes by re-running the link-checker tool post-updates.

**What changed:** External links in the Mission Control Dashboard will be audited, prioritized, and corrected.
