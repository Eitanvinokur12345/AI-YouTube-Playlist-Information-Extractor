# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-712` (dept) · 2026-07-30T19:03:44.107686+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the live inventory of Mission Control Dashboard links.
2. Anchor generates a report identifying broken, redirected, or outdated external links.
3. Anchor prioritizes the report based on impact (e.g., high-traffic pages, critical dependencies).
4. Anchor submits the finalized prioritized report to LinLea for review.
5. LinLea approves the report and assigns remediation tasks to the relevant team.
6. Team executes fixes (e.g., updating URLs, removing dead links) within the agreed timeline.

**What changed:** External links in the Mission Control Dashboard will be systematically audited and prioritized for remediation.
