**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to identify broken or outdated links.
2. Generate a prioritized report of all flagged links with remediation steps.
3. Deliver the report to the lead by EOD.
4. Schedule a follow-up review of remediation progress within 48 hours.
5. Update the department’s link inventory with corrections post-remediation.
6. Document lessons learned for future link maintenance.

**What changed:** Broken/outdated links will be systematically identified and remediated with clear accountability.
