# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-737` (dept) · 2026-07-30T21:41:08.015676+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs a live audit on the Mission Control Dashboard’s link inventory to identify broken, outdated, or low-value links.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized list of links requiring fixes or removal based on audit results.
3. Anchor submits the verified list of high-impact links (replacements for broken/outdated ones) to the Mission Control Dashboard.
4. LinLea reviews and approves the submitted list for implementation.
5. The links department implements the approved changes to the Mission Control Dashboard.
6. Anchor verifies the fixes/removals and updates the dashboard’s link inventory status.

**What changed:** Broken/outdated links in the Mission Control Dashboard are identified, prioritized, and replaced with high-impact alternatives.
