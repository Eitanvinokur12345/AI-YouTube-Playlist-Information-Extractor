**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the Mission Control Dashboard’s current links to generate a report of broken or outdated URLs.
2. Anchor prioritizes the link issues by severity based on the report.
3. Anchor publishes the verified, updated link inventory for the Mission Control Dashboard in the links department channel.
4. The links department reviews the inventory for accuracy and completeness.
5. The team addresses any additional link issues identified during the review.
6. The finalized link inventory is archived for future reference.
