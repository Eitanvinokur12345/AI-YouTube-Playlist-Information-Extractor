# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-780` (dept) · 2026-07-29T20:31:30.453415+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to identify broken/outdated links.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report of all bad links, ranked by severity.
3. Anchor delivers the report to the team for immediate prioritization and fixes.
4. The team reviews the report and assigns fixes based on severity rankings.
5. Anchor monitors progress and updates the report as links are resolved.
6. The team conducts a follow-up check to confirm all links are functional.

**What changed:** A systematic, tool-driven approach to identify and prioritize broken/outdated links for immediate team action.
