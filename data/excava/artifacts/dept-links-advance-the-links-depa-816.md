**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the Mission Control Dashboard’s live link inventory.
2. Anchor generates a prioritized report of broken, redirected, or outdated external links.
3. Anchor shares the report with the team for immediate review.
4. Team reviews the report and assigns fixes to relevant members.
5. Team addresses high-priority broken links within 48 hours.
6. Anchor updates the Mission Control Dashboard with corrected links post-fix.

**What changed:** External links in the Mission Control Dashboard will be systematically audited and corrected.
