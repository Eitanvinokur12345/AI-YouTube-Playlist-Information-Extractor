**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool on the department’s current link inventory to generate a fresh report of broken links.
2. Anchor compiles and shares the verified list of active links alongside the broken link report.
3. The department reviews the prioritized list of dead/redirected links for immediate action.
4. Assign owners to address each broken link (update, remove, or replace).
5. Implement fixes and verify corrections using the link-checker tool.
6. Document the updated link inventory and archive the broken link report for tracking.

**What changed:** Department now has a prioritized, actionable list of broken links and a verified inventory of active links.
