# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-847` (dept) · 2026-07-28T23:26:22.006307+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to identify all broken links.
2. Generate a prioritized report listing exact failed URLs, their dead status, and locations in the department’s inventory.
3. Distribute the report to the relevant teams for immediate review.
4. Schedule weekly re-checks to monitor progress and catch new broken links.
5. Update the inventory with corrections based on the report’s findings.
6. Document the process for future link maintenance.

**What changed:** Broken links will now be systematically identified, prioritized, and addressed.
