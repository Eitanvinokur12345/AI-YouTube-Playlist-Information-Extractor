**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to identify broken or outdated links.
2. Anchor compiles a prioritized report of broken/outdated links, including status and severity.
3. Anchor delivers the report to the team for immediate review.
4. Team reviews the report and assigns action items for link fixes.
5. Team schedules follow-up to address high-severity issues within 48 hours.
6. Anchor updates the department’s link inventory post-fixes.
