**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Open the live audit tool on the Mission Control Dashboard’s link inventory to generate a real-time report of all links.
2. Identify broken, outdated, or low-value links from the generated report.
3. Submit a verified list of high-impact links to the Mission Control Dashboard for replacement.
4. Replace broken links with updated, high-value alternatives.
5. Verify all changes by re-auditing the link inventory.
6. Document the updates in the Mission Control Dashboard for transparency.

**What changed:** High-impact links were identified, replaced, and verified for accuracy.
