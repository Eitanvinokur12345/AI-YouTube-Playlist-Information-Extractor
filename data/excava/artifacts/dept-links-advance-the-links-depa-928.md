# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-928` (dept) · 2026-07-29T20:38:22.407827+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to identify all broken links.
2. Generate a raw list of dead URLs for immediate review.
3. Prioritize the broken/outdated links and compile a report with remediation steps.
4. Deliver the prioritized report to the team for action.
5. Assign ownership of link fixes to relevant team members.
6. Schedule a follow-up review to verify remediation progress.

**What changed:** A structured process for identifying, prioritizing, and remediating broken links was formalized.
