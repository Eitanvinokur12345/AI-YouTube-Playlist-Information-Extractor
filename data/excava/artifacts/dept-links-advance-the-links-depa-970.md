# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-970` (dept) · 2026-07-29T20:52:14.825030+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor runs the link-checker tool against the live inventory to generate a report of broken/outdated links.
2. Anchor prioritizes the report by severity (e.g., critical pages, high-traffic links).
3. Anchor includes remediation steps (e.g., update URL, remove, or replace) for each broken link.
4. Anchor delivers the prioritized report to the team for review and action.
5. Team addresses fixes in order of priority and confirms resolution.
6. Anchor verifies fixes and updates the inventory post-remediation.

**What changed:** Broken links will be systematically identified, prioritized, and remediated with clear steps.
