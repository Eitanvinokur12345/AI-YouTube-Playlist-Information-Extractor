# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-996` (dept) · 2026-07-28T07:21:14.022329+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Anchor compiles and delivers a prioritized report of broken/outdated links and removal candidates, with clear reasons and trade-offs for each category.
2. Team reviews the report to validate findings and assess impact of proposed changes.
3. Prioritize fixes for broken links and removal of redundant/outdated links based on report recommendations.
4. Implement corrections and removals in batches, documenting changes for transparency.
5. Update internal documentation to reflect the revised link inventory.
6. Schedule a quarterly review cycle to maintain link integrity.

**What changed:** A structured, data-driven approach to link management with clear accountability and review cycles.
