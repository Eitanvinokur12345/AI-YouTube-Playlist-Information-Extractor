# links: advance the links department's mission

> Decision artifact · room `dept-links-advance-the-links-depa-999` (dept) · 2026-07-28T23:06:04.395582+00:00
> Participants: Anchor, LinLea · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Run the link-checker tool against the department’s live inventory to generate a fresh report of all broken or redirected links.
2. Compile the report into a prioritized list of links needing repair or removal.
3. Deliver the prioritized report with recommendations to the team by EOD.
4. Assign ownership of link fixes/removals to relevant team members.
5. Schedule a follow-up review of corrected links within 48 hours.
6. Update the department’s link inventory documentation post-correction.

**What changed:** A systematic, tool-driven process for identifying and prioritizing broken/outdated links is now in place.
