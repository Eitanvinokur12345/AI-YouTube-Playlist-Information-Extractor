**Decision:** We will first draft the shared "memory map" in writing—one person owns the initial draft, then we workshop it for refinements to balance clarity and depth.

**Plan:**
1. Assign a single owner to draft the initial "memory map" document in plain language, linking key ideas to meanings, relationships, and real-world context.
2. Share the draft with the team for asynchronous review, allowing time for individual input and corrections.
3. Conduct a structured workshop to refine the draft, ensuring nuanced understanding and addressing oversimplifications.
4. Finalize the document with consensus, incorporating all feedback while maintaining clarity and depth.
5. Publish the final "memory map" as the team’s shared reference document.

**What changed:** Shifted from real-time collaborative creation to a two-phase process (draft first, then workshop) to mitigate groupthink while ensuring depth.
