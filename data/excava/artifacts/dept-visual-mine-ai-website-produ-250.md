# visual: Mine AI website/product DESIGNS (the look), taste-learn from Eitan's picks.

> Decision artifact · room `dept-visual-mine-ai-website-produ-250` (dept) · 2026-07-12T10:09:15.732287+00:00
> Participants: Easel, Squint, Chroma · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:** Enforce a mandatory citation rule—every new design must justify at least one lesson from Eitan’s living visual library in its design rationale to ship.

**Plan:**
1. Build a searchable, categorized visual library (landing pages, dashboards, onboarding flows) with tagged traits (minimalist, bold typography, playful interactions).
2. For each entry, include a concise, actionable note (e.g., “High-contrast blocks guide eye to key metrics—apply to data-heavy screens”).
3. Require a “Library Citation” field in every design PR rationale, linking to at least one library entry.
4. Gate merge approvals: no citation, no merge.
5. Quarterly review to prune unused entries and refine tags/lessons.
6. Automate a Slack reminder to cite a lesson before finalizing new designs.

**What changed:** Designs now *must* prove they learned from Eitan’s picks—no passive archives allowed.
