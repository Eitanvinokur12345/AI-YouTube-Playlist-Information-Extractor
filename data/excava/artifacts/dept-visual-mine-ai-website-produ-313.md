**Decision:** Use a muted teal accent against a clean white background to create a trustworthy and approachable AI interface.
**Plan:**
1. Set the primary background color to a clean white or very light grey to ensure readability and neutrality.
2. Use a muted teal as the accent color for interactive elements such as buttons, hover states, and alerts to add a subtle pop of color.
3. Ensure the contrast between the background and accent color is sufficient for users with visual impairments.
4. Apply the accent color consistently across the interface to create a cohesive and recognizable brand identity.
5. Test the color scheme with a diverse group of users to validate its effectiveness in conveying approachability and sophistication.
**What changed:** The initial proposal of using a dark grey background with neon accents was replaced with a lighter and more approachable color scheme.
