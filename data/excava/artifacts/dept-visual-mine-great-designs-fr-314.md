**Decision:**
Chroma will synthesize the debate by executing the most actionable and high-value plan to gather visual design artifacts for the hub.

**Plan:**
1. Open Playwright MCP and navigate to Dribbble’s "SaaS" or "Dashboard" category.
2. Capture a high-quality screenshot of a well-designed SaaS dashboard (e.g., from a reputable designer like [@byq](https://dribbble.com/byq) or [@adhamdannaway](https://dribbble.com/adhamdannaway)).
3. Save the screenshot with a descriptive filename (e.g., `saas-dashboard-pattern-001.png`) in the hub’s `/taste-materials/` directory.
4. Repeat step 2-3 for an "Agent Platform" interface (e.g., from [@mengto](https://dribbble.com/mengto) or [@nordwood](https://dribbble.com/nordwood)).
5. Organize screenshots into subfolders by category (e.g., `/saas-dashboards/`, `/agent-platforms/`).
6. Add a `README.md` in `/taste-materials/` with sourcing credits and usage notes.

**What changed:**
Prioritized capturing both SaaS dashboards *and* agent platform interfaces as distinct taste artifacts, ensuring broader design pattern coverage.
