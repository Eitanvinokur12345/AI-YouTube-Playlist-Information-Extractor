**Plan:**
1. Build two prototypes: one with a fixed-height timeline strip at the top, and one with a collapsible thin indicator that expands on hover/click.
2. Recruit 10–15 users per prototype for live testing, measuring scrolling behavior and clarity metrics.
3. Track time-to-completion for key tasks (e.g., locating playback mark, interpreting dense data).
4. Log user feedback on visibility, liveliness, and enjoyment via post-task surveys.
5. Analyze data to compare scrolling frequency, task accuracy, and subjective ratings between versions.
6. Iterate based on findings, finalizing the version with superior clarity and speed metrics.
