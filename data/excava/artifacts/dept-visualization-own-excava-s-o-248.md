**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Integrate a real-time contrast checker into the design interface that flashes red on violations as designers work.
2. Add a persistent "contrast debt" tracker in the interface, visible at all times, listing unresolved violations.
3. Ensure the tracker updates dynamically—no manual refresh—so the team sees progress or stagnation immediately.
4. Design the tracker to show severity (e.g., WCAG levels) and quick-fix buttons (e.g., "Adjust to AA").
5. Log violations in a shared team view (e.g., Slack/email digest) for accountability without blocking workflows.
6. Include a "contrast debt" counter in the interface header to reinforce ownership at a glance.

**What changed:**
Live feedback + persistent debt tracking replace blockers and noise with ownership and clarity.
