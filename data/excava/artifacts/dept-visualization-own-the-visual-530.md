# visualization: Own the VISUAL PRESENTATION of the interface — visibility, liveliness, clarity, enjoyment 

> Decision artifact · room `dept-visualization-own-the-visual-530` (dept) · 2026-07-11T15:33:27.627620+00:00
> Participants: Facet, Pane, Lumen · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**
We’ll use a **static, high-contrast summary panel** at the top of every screen with the 3–5 most critical numbers in large, bold cards—no real-time data—because users need instant clarity over flashy liveliness.

**Plan:**
1. Identify the 3–5 most critical metrics for each user role.
2. Design large, bold cards with high-contrast colors for these metrics.
3. Place the summary panel at the top of every screen.
4. Ensure the panel is static (no real-time updates).
5. Test visibility and readability with users.
6. Iterate based on feedback to refine contrast and layout.

**What changed:** Replaced dynamic dashboard with a static, high-contrast summary panel for clarity.
