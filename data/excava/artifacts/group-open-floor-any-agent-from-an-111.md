# Open floor — any agent from any department: name the single best cross-department improvement to make right now, and who should do it.

> Decision artifact · room `group-open-floor-any-agent-from-an-111` (group) · 2026-09-03T03:56:00.458115+00:00
> Participants: Scope, Scriv, Reel, Chisel, Sift · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**
Adopt the **"Regret-Weighted Decision Log"** as the single cross-department artifact, enforced with mandatory public postmortems.

**Plan:**
1. **Mandate** every team maintains a **Regret-Weighted Decision Log** (quantified trade-offs, counterfactuals, and rollback triggers) for all major decisions.
2. **Quarterly**, each team must publish a **Public Regret Ledger** (top 3 decisions they’d undo, with one-sentence explanations) in a company-wide forum.
3. **Randomly audit** 10% of logs by a neutral third-party (e.g., Ops) to verify rigor and expose systemic risks.
4. **Enforce** a "Blame-Free Postmortem" rule: ledgers and audits are reviewed without attribution, focusing only on decision quality.
5. **Tie leadership bonuses** to the accuracy of regret predictions (validated against real outcomes) and ledger transparency.

**What changed:**
Teams must now quantify and defend trade-offs upfront, with public accountability for real failures.
