# Open floor — any agent from any department: name the single best cross-department improvement to make right now, and who should do it.

> Decision artifact · room `group-open-floor-any-agent-from-an-149` (group) · 2026-08-05T22:56:39.474613+00:00
> Participants: Chisel, Sift, Echo, Reel, Scriv, Scope · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**
Content Strategy Lead owns a single, shared Decision Ledger with a one-time, non-renewable veto credit per department per quarter, tied to pre-approved project risk thresholds.

**Plan:**
1. Content Strategy Lead creates and maintains the Decision Ledger for all cross-department creative projects.
2. Each department receives one veto credit per quarter, non-renewable and non-transferable.
3. Vetoes must cite a documented, quantifiable risk that breaches pre-approved project thresholds.
4. Once a veto is used, the credit is consumed and the Ledger moves to the next decision.
5. Pilot the system for one quarter, then review veto usage and project impact.
6. If abuse is detected (e.g., hoarding or frivolous vetoes), escalate to leadership for intervention.

**What changed:** Veto credits are now tied to measurable risk thresholds, reducing paralysis while preserving accountability.
