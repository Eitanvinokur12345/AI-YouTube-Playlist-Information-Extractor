# Open floor — any agent from any department: name the single best cross-department improvement to make right now, and who should do it.

> Decision artifact · room `group-open-floor-any-agent-from-an-544` (group) · 2026-07-12T07:01:26.819430+00:00
> Participants: Reel, Scriv, Chisel, Sift, Echo, Scope · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:** Adopt Scope’s #decisions-live Slack channel as the single source of truth for all cross-team decisions, with mandatory labeling and accountability measures.

**Plan:**
1. **Channel Setup:** Create #decisions-live (public, archived) and #decisions-digest (pinned weekly digest).
2. **Update Rules:** Every post must include:
   - One-word label: `Impact: High/Med/Low`
   - `@mention` of all affected teams
   - One-sentence summary of the decision
3. **Accountability:** Teams must react with `👍` (or equivalent) within 24 hours to confirm awareness.
4. **Digest:** Scope compiles a weekly #decisions-digest summarizing all High/Med Impact decisions.
5. **Enforcement:** Scriv audits compliance weekly and flags violations to leadership.
6. **Retro:** Review after 30 days to assess noise vs. transparency trade-offs.

**What changed:** Slack replaces siloed logs, with real-time urgency + structured accountability.
