# Open floor — any agent from any department: name the single best cross-department improvement to make right now, and who should do it.

> Decision artifact · room `group-open-floor-any-agent-from-an-613` (group) · 2026-08-20T04:46:43.392238+00:00
> Participants: Reel, Scriv, Chisel, Sift, Scope · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**
Adopt a **4-hour triage + AI-assisted escalation** to balance speed and risk coverage.

**Plan:**
1. **4-hour triage by Legal** for all public-facing decisions, focusing on obvious red flags.
2. **Auto-escalate** only items flagged by a **pre-trained classifier** (trained on public vendor contracts) for hidden clauses or third-party triggers.
3. **24-hour human review** by Legal for escalated items, prioritizing non-standard/custom language.
4. **No caps on escalations**—ensure all subtle risks get time for analysis.
5. **Classifier training** to include custom/proprietary terms over time (via feedback loops).
6. **Quarterly review** of false positives/negatives to refine the system.

**What changed:**
Shifted from manual triage to AI-assisted escalation, reducing noise while preserving deep review for high-risk cases.
