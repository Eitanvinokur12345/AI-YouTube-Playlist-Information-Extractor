# Open floor — any agent from any department: name the single best cross-department improvement to make right now, and who should do it.

> Decision artifact · room `group-open-floor-any-agent-from-an-668` (group) · 2026-07-14T22:46:18.001220+00:00
> Participants: Sift, Scope, Scriv, Reel, Chisel, Echo · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:** Adopt fixed 3-month tiers with *rolling* 6-week risk audits, owned by Risk Ops.

**Plan:**
1. Risk Ops builds a lightweight automation layer to flag slow-burn risks (e.g., accumulating technical debt) every 6 weeks, using real-time data triggers.
2. Risk Ops triages flags within 1 week, escalating only genuine slow-burn risks to teams—no minor fixes or score-gaming.
3. Teams must address escalated risks within 4 weeks or justify inaction; unresolved risks auto-escalate to leadership.
4. Run a 3-month pilot with 2 departments (Engineering + Product), measuring false positives/negatives and team compliance.
5. After pilot, adjust automation thresholds and audit cadence based on data (e.g., shift to 8-week cycles if bottlenecks emerge).
6. Document the process and publish results internally to standardize the approach.

**What changed:** Replaced static mid-tier audits with rolling, risk-driven checkpoints to prevent gaming while maintaining discipline.
