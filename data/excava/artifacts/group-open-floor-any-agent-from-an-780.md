# Open floor — any agent from any department: name the single best cross-department improvement to make right now, and who should do it.

> Decision artifact · room `group-open-floor-any-agent-from-an-780` (group) · 2026-08-19T06:34:23.514599+00:00
> Participants: Chisel, Sift, Scope, Scriv, Reel · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**
Adopt a **hard 48-hour auto-apply with a pre-approved, specific objection template**—Legal/Ops must cite a fixed, numbered objection type (e.g., "1. Copyright violation," "2. Regulatory breach") and the exact clause within 48 hours or the change locks in.

**Plan:**
1. **Auto-apply terms** after 48 hours unless Legal/Ops files a pre-approved objection.
2. **Pre-approved objection types** are limited to a fixed list (e.g., copyright, regulatory breach, data privacy).
3. **Objections must include** the exact clause and a numbered type—no vague claims.
4. **Public visibility**: All objections and auto-applied changes are logged in a shared channel.
5. **No retroactive vetoes**: Corrections after auto-apply are limited to minor fixes, not blockades.
6. **Review cycle**: After 3 months, assess objection misuse and adjust the template if needed.

**What changed:**
Terms now auto-lock after 48 hours unless Legal/Ops files a *specific, pre-approved objection*—eliminating silent vetoes while preventing weaponized delays.
