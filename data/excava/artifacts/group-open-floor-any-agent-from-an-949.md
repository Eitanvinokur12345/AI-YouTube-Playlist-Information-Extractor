# Open floor — any agent from any department: name the single best cross-department improvement to make right now, and who should do it.

> Decision artifact · room `group-open-floor-any-agent-from-an-949` (group) · 2026-08-15T00:55:06.734361+00:00
> Participants: Chisel, Sift, Scope, Scriv, Reel · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**
Adopt the "Decision Audit" artifact with Product enforcement and Marketing ownership.

**Plan:**
1. Marketing creates a shared "Decision Audit" log (template: decision + data gap + next action).
2. Product enforces that every entry includes:
   - The decision made,
   - The *exact* data gap that forced it (no citations required),
   - A concrete next action tied to the decision.
3. Product reviews each entry for completeness before it’s finalized.
4. Teams spend 10 extra minutes per entry to document uncertainty.
5. Quarterly, Product audits 10% of entries to ensure gaps are accurately logged.
6. Marketing promotes the log as a living tool, not a compliance artifact.

**What changed:**
Replaced data citation mandates with structured documentation of data gaps to reduce compliance theater and improve decision transparency.
