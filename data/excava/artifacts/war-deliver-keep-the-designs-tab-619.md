# Deliver: Keep the Designs tab pure: designs only, live previews, taste-ranked

> Decision artifact · room `war-deliver-keep-the-designs-tab-619` (war) · 2026-07-31T10:33:53.748913+00:00
> Participants: Reel, Scriv, Chisel, Sift, Scope, Echo · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Publish a short, public checklist of taste rules (e.g., no nudity, no watermarks, no extreme blur) with clear examples.
2. Require submitters to self-grade against the checklist before uploading, marking “pass” or “fail” for each criterion.
3. Apply machine verification for objective criteria (e.g., blur detection, watermark scan) to auto-approve submissions that pass both self-grade and machine checks.
4. Route submissions flagged by self-grade or machine checks to a 30-second human triage for final approval.
5. Display live previews of auto-approved designs in the Designs tab, ranked by community taste signals (e.g., likes, saves).
6. Monitor rejection rates and adjust the checklist or machine thresholds quarterly based on creator feedback and quality trends.

**What changed:** Self-grading + machine checks replace manual review for most submissions, reducing queue backlog while preserving quality.
