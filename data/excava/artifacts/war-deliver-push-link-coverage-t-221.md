2. **Flag and prioritize links** based on their impact, frequency of access, and compliance requirements.
3. **Pair flagged links with focused transcript sprints** to ensure immediate value and progress towards the goal.
4. **Run parallel audits** to continually refine the link landscape understanding and adjust priorities as needed.
5. **Monitor progress and adjust the approach** to maintain the +5%/day pace and ensure even progress across the link landscape.
**What changed:** The approach now combines a lightweight scan with a tiered, prioritized, and focused sprint methodology to balance speed and comprehensiveness.
