**Decision:** Rotate the Decision Ledger ownership weekly among senior contributors to avoid bottlenecks and keep link coverage moving at +5%/day.

**Plan:**
1. Assign the Content Strategy Lead to pilot the *shared Decision Ledger* for the first week, enforcing strict updates to establish the process.
2. After the pilot, rotate ownership weekly among senior contributors (e.g., Engineering, Product, and Design leads) to distribute risk and maintain momentum.
3. Automate a lightweight log from existing tools (e.g., ticket statuses) to supplement manual updates, reducing administrative burden.
4. Require all contributors to update the ledger in real-time or within 24 hours of a decision, with veto power reserved for measurable risks only.
5. Conduct a weekly review to flag stalled decisions and redistribute ownership if coverage stalls.
6. Publish the ledger publicly (e.g., GitHub) to ensure transparency and prevent duplicate work.
