# Deliver: Push link coverage toward 100% at +5%/day (the access gate)

> Decision artifact · room `war-deliver-push-link-coverage-t-634` (war) · 2026-08-13T11:23:51.527082+00:00
> Participants: Reel, Scriv, Chisel, Sift, Scope, Echo · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:** Adopt the "Decision Ledger" artifact where every entry must link to a verifiable artifact proving execution, not just planning.

**Plan:**
1. Require all teams to create a "Decision Ledger" entry for every key choice, including a link to a verifiable artifact (e.g., PR, config change, or meeting notes).
2. Enforce a mandatory next-action owner and deadline for each entry to ensure accountability.
3. Conduct weekly audits to verify artifact links and remove stale entries lacking proof of execution.
4. Introduce a lightweight template for "Decision Ledger" entries to standardize format and reduce upfront effort.
5. Tie link coverage metrics to team performance reviews to incentivize compliance.
6. Provide tooling (e.g., GitHub issue templates) to automate artifact linking and tracking.

**What changed:** Decision artifacts now require verifiable execution proof, directly driving link coverage toward 100%.
