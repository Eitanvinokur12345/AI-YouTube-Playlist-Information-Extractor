# Deliver: Push link coverage toward 100% at +5%/day (the access gate)

> Decision artifact · room `war-deliver-push-link-coverage-t-869` (war) · 2026-08-25T06:57:52.273603+00:00
> Participants: Reel, Scriv, Chisel, Sift, Scope, Echo · synthesized by mistral/mistral-small-latest

**Decision:**

**Plan:**
1. Allocate 1% of daily link coverage effort to the top 20% most visible pages to rapidly open the access gate.
2. Allocate the remaining 4% of daily effort to a site-wide sweep, prioritizing pages by traffic and risk (e.g., legal terms, checkout flows).
3. Use automated tooling to identify and fix broken links, logging changes in a shared tracker.
4. Assign Scriv as the owner to coordinate daily execution, review progress, and escalate blockers.
5. Publish a daily dashboard showing coverage growth and critical failures resolved.
6. Upon reaching 100% coverage, archive the tracker and close the bus task.

**What changed:** Shifted from a tiered page split to a 1% fast-gate + 4% risk-prioritized sweep to minimize undetected critical failures.
