**Decision:** Implement a hybrid approach to achieve +5% daily coverage 
1. **Start manual crawl**: Perform a daily 30-minute manual crawl of the top 50 most-linked pages in the repo to immediately lock in +5% daily coverage.
2. **Develop automated scraper**: Prioritize the development of a simple, automated scraper that logs every decision, ensuring audibility and reliability.
3. **Transition to automated scraper**: Hand over the coverage process to the automated scraper once it's reviewed and refined, to ensure long-term sustainability.
4. **Monitor and refine**: Continuously monitor the automated scraper's performance and refine it as needed to maintain accuracy and reliability.
5. **Review coverage metrics**: Regularly review coverage metrics to identify areas for improvement and adjust the approach as necessary.
**What changed:** Manual crawl provides immediate coverage, while automated scraper ensures long-term reliability and accuracy.
