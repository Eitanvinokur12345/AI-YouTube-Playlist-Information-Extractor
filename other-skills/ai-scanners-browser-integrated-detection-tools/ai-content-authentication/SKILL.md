# AI Content Authentication

## Overview
The ability to identify and verify whether digital content (video, audio, images) has been generated or significantly altered by Artificial Intelligence, often by detecting embedded digital fingerprints or anomalies, even if metadata has been stripped.

**Use case:** Verifying the authenticity of media, combating misinformation, identifying AI-generated deepfakes.
