# Human-in-the-Loop Data Collection

## Overview
Utilizing human workers to perform tasks while being recorded, providing vast amounts of real-world, first-person perspective data for AI model training, especially for tasks that are difficult for current AI to learn autonomously.

**Use case:** Collecting diverse and nuanced data for AI systems that require understanding of human behavior, interaction with environments, and complex physical tasks.
