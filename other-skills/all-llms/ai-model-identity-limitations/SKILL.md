# Understanding AI model identity and limitations

## Overview
Knowledge of how AI models are trained, how they 'identify' themselves, and the potential for 'model collapse' or 'Habsburg AI' due to self-referential training data.

**Use case:** Informing AI development, research, and responsible deployment by recognizing inherent limitations in model self-awareness and data lineage.

## Key steps
1. Recognize that AI models do not possess true self-awareness or identity.
2. Be aware that models trained on data generated by other models can lead to degradation in quality and accuracy over time (Habsburg AI).
3. Consider the source and composition of training data when evaluating model behavior.
