# Critical evaluation of AI output

## Overview
The ability to critically assess and verify the information provided by AI models, especially regarding their self-identification or factual claims.

**Use case:** Evaluating the reliability of AI-generated responses and understanding potential biases or inaccuracies.

## Key steps
1. Always cross-reference AI-generated information with reliable external sources.
2. Be aware that AI models may 'hallucinate' or confidently provide incorrect information.
3. Understand that an AI's self-identification is based on its training data, not an inherent 'identity'.
