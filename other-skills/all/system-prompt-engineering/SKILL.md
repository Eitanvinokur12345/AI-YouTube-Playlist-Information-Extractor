# System Prompt Engineering

## Overview
The ability to understand, adapt, and create system prompts that guide AI models on how to think, respond, and what to avoid, effectively customizing their behavior for specific tasks.

**Use case:** Customizing AI behavior, improving AI performance, replicating specific AI functionalities.

## Key steps
1. Study leaked system prompts from major AI models to understand best practices.
2. Experiment with different system prompt structures to achieve desired AI responses.
3. Iterate on system prompts based on AI output and performance.
