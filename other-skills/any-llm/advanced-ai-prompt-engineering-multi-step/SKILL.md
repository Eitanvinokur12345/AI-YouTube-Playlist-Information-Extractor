# Advanced AI Prompt Engineering for Multi-step Tasks

## Overview
Ability to structure complex tasks for AI, breaking them down into multiple logical steps, providing detailed instructions, maintaining consistency, and specifying output requirements for a cohesive, multi-part output.

**Use case:** Automating complex workflows, generating comprehensive reports, managing multi-stage content creation.

## Key steps
1. Break down the task into clear, logical steps before starting.
2. Execute each step one at a time in order.
3. Do not skip any steps.
4. Maintain consistency in style, format, and tone across all steps.
5. If something is unclear, make a reasonable assumption and proceed.
6. Keep the output clean, structured, and easy to follow.
7. Highlight key results or final outputs clearly.
8. Avoid unnecessary explanations unless needed.
9. Ensure the task is a single continuous task; do not treat steps as separate responses.
10. Complete everything in one cohesive output.
