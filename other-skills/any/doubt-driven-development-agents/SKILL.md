# Doubt-Driven Development for Agents

## Overview
A technique where a primary AI agent makes a claim, and a second agent (with clean context) attacks or validates that claim to improve reliability and self-correction.

**Use case:** Enhancing the robustness, accuracy, and critical thinking capabilities of AI agent outputs.
