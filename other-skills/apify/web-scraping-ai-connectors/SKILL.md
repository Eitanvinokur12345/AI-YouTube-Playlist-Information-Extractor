# Web Scraping with AI Connectors

## Overview
Integrating web scraping tools (like Apify) with AI assistants (like Claude) to pull real-time data from job boards and other websites for analysis.

**Use case:** Collecting up-to-date job postings, gathering market intelligence, automating data extraction.

## Key steps
1. Ensure the connector is properly installed and toggled on in the AI assistant.
2. Specify the target websites or platforms for scraping (e.g., LinkedIn, Google Jobs).
3. Be aware of usage limits and terms of service for scraping tools.
