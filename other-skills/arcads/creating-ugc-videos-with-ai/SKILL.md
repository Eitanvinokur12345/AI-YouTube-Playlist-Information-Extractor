# Creating UGC Videos with AI

## Overview
Leveraging AI models like Seedance 2.0 to generate realistic user-generated content (UGC) videos for product promotion, including replacing products, adapting styles, and adding voiceovers.

**Use case:** Generating marketing content, cloning competitor ads, creating product showcases.

## Key steps
1. Use detailed prompts to guide AI for specific actions (e.g., wearing a jacket, putting on glasses).
2. Reference existing images or videos to provide context and maintain consistency in AI-generated content.
3. Utilize natural language for prompting, no coding skills required.
4. Consider editing minor flaws in AI-generated videos using traditional editing software or regenerating with refined prompts.
