# Dolly Zoom Effect with AI

## Overview
Creating a dolly zoom effect by generating two distinct images (wide-angle and zoomed-in) and then using an AI video model to transition between them, simulating a physical camera dolly forward.

**Use case:** Cinematic video production, social media content creation, visual storytelling.

## Key steps
1. Use a wide-angle shot for the start frame and a telephoto shot for the end frame.
2. Ensure the subject is centered and stable in both shots.
3. Utilize specific prompts to guide the AI for physical camera movement rather than digital zoom.
