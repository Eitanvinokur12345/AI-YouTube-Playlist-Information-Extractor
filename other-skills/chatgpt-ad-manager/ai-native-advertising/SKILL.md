# AI-native advertising

## Overview
The ability to create, manage, and optimize advertising campaigns within AI platforms like ChatGPT, leveraging their unique conversational and contextual capabilities.

**Use case:** Reaching customers directly within AI-generated responses based on their queries and intent.

## Key steps
1. Identify keywords that indicate high purchase intent or problem-solving needs.
2. Start with a small test budget to understand performance.
3. Monitor impressions, conversions, and clicks to optimize campaigns.

- **Category:** marketing
