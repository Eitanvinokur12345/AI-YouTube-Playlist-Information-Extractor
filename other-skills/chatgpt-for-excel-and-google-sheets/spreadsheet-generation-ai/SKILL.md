# Spreadsheet Generation with AI

## Overview
Using natural language prompts to generate full spreadsheets, including tabs, formulas, and data structures, for various business needs like budgeting, expense tracking, and savings.

**Use case:** Automating the creation of complex financial models, budget templates, and data analysis sheets without manual formula input.

## Key steps
1. Be specific with your prompt to get the desired spreadsheet structure.
2. Specify categories for budgets, expenses, and savings.
3. Mention the desired time frame (e.g., annual, monthly).
