# AI Data Privacy Management

## Overview
Understanding and managing how personal data and chat interactions are used, stored, and shared by AI models and platforms, including disabling model improvement, managing shared links, and understanding data retention policies.

**Use case:** Ensuring personal information remains private when interacting with AI chatbots and preventing unintended public exposure or use for model training.

## Key steps
1. Disable 'Improve the model for everyone' in data controls.
2. Regularly check and delete shared links.
3. Be aware that deleted chats and temporary chats may be retained for up to 30 days or longer due to legal orders.
