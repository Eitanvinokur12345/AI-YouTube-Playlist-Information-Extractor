# AI-powered Interior Design

## Overview
Utilizing AI to generate redesigned room concepts based on an uploaded image and specific style preferences.

**Use case:** Redesigning living spaces, visualizing new decor, furniture, and layouts.

## Key steps
1. Provide clear instructions on desired style (e.g., warm, modern, realistic).
2. Specify elements to keep or change (e.g., 'keep basic furniture', 'different layout, colors, lighting, decor').
3. Upload a high-quality photo of the room.
