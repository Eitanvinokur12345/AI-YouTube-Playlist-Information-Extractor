# AI Prompt Engineering for Website Generation

## Overview
Crafting detailed and specific prompts for AI models to generate high-quality, targeted website content and structure, incorporating business details and customer feedback.

**Use case:** Automating the creation of tailored website content and design briefs.

## Key steps
1. Include desired tone, style, and key selling points.
2. Specify sections and calls-to-action for the website.
3. Provide examples or source material (like reviews) for context.
