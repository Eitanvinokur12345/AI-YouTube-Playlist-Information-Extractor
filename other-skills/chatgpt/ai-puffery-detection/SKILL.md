# Detecting AI Puffery

## Overview
Identifying AI-generated text by its tendency to 'puff up' the importance of subjects, using words like 'vital', 'significant', 'crucial', 'pivotal', 'enduring', 'lasting', and editorializing facts rather than just stating them.

**Use case:** Identifying AI-generated content in articles, essays, or reports.

## Key steps
1. Look for excessive use of adjectives and adverbs that overstate importance.
2. Watch for phrases that describe places or concepts in overly grand or 'breathtaking' terms.
3. AI tends to editorialize facts, constantly and positively, rather than presenting them neutrally.
