# Identifying AI Vocabulary

## Overview
Recognizing specific words that became much more frequent in text after 2022, often overused by LLMs, such as 'delve', 'tapestry', 'intricate', 'underscore', 'additionally', 'boasts', 'bolstered', 'crucial', 'enduring', 'enhance', 'emphasizing', 'fostering', 'garner', 'highlight', 'interplay', 'key', 'landscape', 'meticulously', 'pivotal', 'showcase', 'testament', 'valuable', 'vibrant'.

**Use case:** Detecting AI-generated content by analyzing word choice and frequency.

## Key steps
1. A high density of these 'AI vocabulary' words in a single piece of writing is a strong indicator.
2. Different AI models may have different favorite words, but a core set is common.
