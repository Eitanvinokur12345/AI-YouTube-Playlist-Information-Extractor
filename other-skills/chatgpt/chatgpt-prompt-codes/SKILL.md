# ChatGPT Prompt Modifier Codes

## Overview
A set of prompt modifier words and phrases that shift ChatGPT behavior: TLDR (compress to shortest version), ELI10 (explain like 10-year-old), Red Team (devil advocacy), Hormozi (business coach persona), /human (remove AI language), Feynman (teach from scratch), Chain (step-by-step reasoning), Contrast (pros/cons), Pressure Test (find flaws), SOS (reduce to essentials).

## Key Techniques
- TLDR — compresses any wall of text to essentials
- ELI10 — explains anything like you are 10 years old
- Red Team — plays devil advocate to make you think smarter
- Hormozi — makes ChatGPT your $10M business coach
- /human — removes AI-sounding language from output
