# ChatGPT Prompt Prefix System

## Overview
A set of 10 single-word or short prefixes that, when pasted before any prompt, activate a specific response mode in ChatGPT (or Claude, Gemini, and other LLMs). Each prefix changes the AI's persona or behavioral mode for specialized outputs.

## Key Techniques
- Single-keyword prefix before the main prompt activates a distinct response mode
- Works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and most LLMs without any system prompt setup
- Combine prefixes for compounded effects (e.g. REDTEAM + SOCRATES)

## How to Apply
Paste the prefix at the very start of your prompt, followed by a space and your actual question or task.

Example: `TRUTHMODE: Is my business idea actually viable or am I just being optimistic?`

## The 10 Prefix Codes

| Prefix | What It Does |
|--------|-------------|
| TRUTHMODE | Stops the AI agreeing with you — gives brutally honest feedback |
| EL10 | Explains the topic as if you are 10 years old |
| REDTEAM | Plays devil's advocate to stress-test your ideas |
| HORMOZI | Responds like a $10M business coach (Alex Hormozi style) |
| HUMAN | Makes the output sound like a real human wrote it |
| FUTUREYOU | Answers as your future, wiser self would |
| UNLEARN | Reveals common misconceptions about your topic |
| 80/20 | Gives the easiest, highest-leverage path to your outcome |
| SOCRATES | Teaches by asking you questions instead of giving direct answers |
| LINDYMODE | Prioritizes long-term proven methods over current hype |

## Examples
- `TRUTHMODE: Review my landing page copy` → harsh, specific critique
- `80/20: How do I learn Python for data analysis?` → fastest path, not exhaustive
- `REDTEAM: Here is my marketing strategy…` → counterarguments and blind spots
- `SOCRATES: I want to understand compound interest` → teaches through questions
