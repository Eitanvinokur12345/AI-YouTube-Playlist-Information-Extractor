# ChatGPT 4-Step Resume Optimization

## Overview
A four-prompt workflow for optimizing resumes against job descriptions: Step 1 gets ATS keyword match score and top 5 missing keywords; Step 2 rewrites experience section with those keywords; Step 3 upgrades passive language to strong action verbs; Step 4 does a final ATS formatting pass to ensure scannability.

## Key Techniques
- Prompt 1: paste resume + job description, ask to act as senior recruiter, get match score and top 5 missing keywords.
- Prompt 2: rewrite experience section to naturally include those keywords without keyword stuffing.
- Prompt 3: upgrade all passive verbs to strong action verbs (managed → led, helped → drove).
- Prompt 4: final ATS formatting pass to ensure clean scannability for automated systems.

## How to Apply
Optimizing a resume for a specific job description to maximize ATS match score and recruiter appeal.
