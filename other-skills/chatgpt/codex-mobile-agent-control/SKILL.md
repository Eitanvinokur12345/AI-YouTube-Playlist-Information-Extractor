# Codex Mobile Agent Control

## Overview
This skill covers the technique of running and managing OpenAI Codex coding agents from the ChatGPT mobile app, enabling you to continue desktop projects, remotely control your computer, and juggle multiple device sessions — all from your phone.

## Key Techniques
- Continue active Codex coding sessions on mobile with the same plugins and environment as your desktop
- Use Codex's computer-use mode to remotely control a laptop/desktop (open browsers, send Slack messages, trigger video creation) from your phone
- Manage multiple machines (e.g., Mac mini + MacBook Pro) in parallel under separate Codex sessions from a single phone

## How to Apply
1. Open the ChatGPT mobile app and activate Codex from within it.
2. Connect your devices — your existing desktop Codex environment (plugins, project context) carries over automatically.
3. To enable remote desktop control: go to Settings and toggle on "Computer Use" and the Codex Chrome Extension.
4. To manage multiple machines simultaneously: go to Settings and toggle on each device you want to attach to.
5. You can now send prompts from your phone that execute tasks on your desktop — opening browsers, sending messages, updating files, or running video tools like Remotion.

## Examples
- You're working on a personal website on your laptop; switch to your phone and continue prompting Codex with the same session context.
- From your phone, tell Codex to open your browser on your Mac mini, draft and send a Slack message, and create a video in Remotion — all without touching the desktop.
- Run one Codex session editing your personal website on your MacBook Pro while another session handles email drafts on your Mac mini.
