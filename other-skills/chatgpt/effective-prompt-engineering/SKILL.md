# Effective Prompt Engineering

## Overview
Crafting detailed and specific prompts for AI models to elicit high-quality, relevant, and accurate responses by providing context, defining roles, specifying audience, stating purpose/outcome, adding constraints (tone, length), including examples, and setting deadlines.

**Use case:** Generating reports, creative writing, code, data analysis, marketing copy, summaries, and more.

## Key steps
1. Provide clear context for the task.
2. Tell the AI what role to play (e.g., 'Act as a marketing expert').
3. Specify the target audience for the output.
4. Clearly state the desired outcome or purpose.
5. Add constraints like desired tone, length, or format.
6. Include examples of the desired output style or content.
7. Set a deadline to imply urgency or efficiency.
