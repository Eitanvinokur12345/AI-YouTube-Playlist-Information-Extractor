# Detecting Fake References (Hallucinations)

## Overview
Identifying AI-generated citations that appear legitimate but are either completely made up, incorrect, or link to non-existent/dead pages. This is a common problem where AI 'hallucinates' sources.

**Use case:** Verifying the authenticity of sources and citations in AI-generated research or articles.

## Key steps
1. Always cross-reference citations with actual sources.
2. Look for broken external links, invalid DOIs or ISBNs, or DOIs that lead to unrelated articles.
