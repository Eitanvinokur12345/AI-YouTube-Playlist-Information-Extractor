# Prompt Engineering for Accuracy

## Overview
Technique to engineer prompts that prioritize factual accuracy, critical analysis, and identification of weaknesses or risks over simple agreement or flattery from an AI model.

**Use case:** Ensuring AI provides unbiased, critical feedback and identifies potential flaws in ideas or plans.

## Key steps
1. Use 'TruthMode' or similar directives to encourage critical thinking.
2. Explicitly ask for counter-arguments, risks, and blind spots.
3. Request separation of facts from opinions.
