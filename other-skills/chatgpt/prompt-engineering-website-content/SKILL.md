# Prompt Engineering for Website Content

## Overview
Crafting detailed and effective prompts for AI chatbots (like ChatGPT) to generate comprehensive website content and design specifications based on existing business information (e.g., customer reviews).

**Use case:** Automating the content creation phase of website development.

## Key steps
1. Include specific sections, design notes, and copy requirements in the prompt.
2. Reference existing customer reviews to ensure content aligns with customer perception.
3. Specify desired output format and variations (e.g., hero sections, CTA styles).
