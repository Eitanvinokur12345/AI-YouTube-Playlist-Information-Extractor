# Human-in-the-Loop AI Workflow

## Overview
Designing AI workflows where human oversight and approval are mandatory before any AI-generated output is finalized or sent to clients.

**Use case:** Reviewing and approving AI-drafted financial reports, commentary, and client communications to ensure accuracy and maintain quality.

## Key steps
1. Create clear approval queues for different types of AI outputs.
2. Provide easy-to-use interfaces for human review, editing, and approval/rejection.
3. Emphasize that AI generates drafts, never final outputs without human consent.
