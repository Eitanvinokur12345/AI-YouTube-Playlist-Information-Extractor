# Building an AI Second Brain

## Overview
The ability to create and manage a personal AI system that stores, organizes, and retrieves information, and can autonomously learn and connect concepts from your data.

**Use case:** Personal knowledge management, automated organization, enhanced decision-making, continuous learning.

## Key steps
1. Integrate an AI model (like Claude) with a note-taking app (like Obsidian) for a basic setup.
2. Implement nightly cleanup routines for autonomous organization and linking of notes.
3. Focus on linking people, companies, and projects mentioned in your notes automatically.
