# CinemaDNA

## Overview
A skill for Codex that teaches an AI agent to think like a director, focusing on cinematic principles for image generation.

**Use case:** Generating cinematic images with specific camera angles, eyelines, and lighting to achieve a movie-like feel.

## Key steps
1. Consider where the camera sits (e.g., wide, medium, close-up).
2. Define who is looking at whom (eyelines).
3. Specify the direction and quality of light.
