# Advanced prompt engineering for personal analysis

## Overview
Crafting detailed and multi-faceted prompts to extract complex personal profiles, identify hidden patterns, and predict future outcomes from an AI's understanding of past conversations.

**Use case:** Deep personal introspection, uncovering unconscious biases or habits, forecasting potential life paths based on current behaviors.

## Key steps
1. Combine multiple aspects of self (personality, goals, fears, insecurities) into a single prompt for a holistic view.
2. Request analysis of 'blind spots' and 'patterns' to encourage the AI to infer beyond explicit statements.
3. Ask for both positive and negative future scenarios ('if I fix it' vs. 'if I don't fix it') to get a balanced perspective.
