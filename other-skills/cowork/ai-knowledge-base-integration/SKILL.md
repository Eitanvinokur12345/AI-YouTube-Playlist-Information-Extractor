# AI Knowledge Base Integration

## Overview
Building and utilizing a persistent knowledge base for AI by uploading business-specific documents (brand voice, past communications, sales scripts) so the AI retains context across interactions and generates consistent, on-brand responses.

**Use case:** Ensuring all AI-generated content adheres to brand guidelines, leverages past successful communications, and understands the business's unique operations without needing repeated explanations.

## Key steps
1. Organize business documents (brand voice, sales scripts, email templates) into projects.
2. Upload relevant files to the AI's knowledge base.
3. Structure information for easy retrieval and understanding by the AI.
4. Regularly update the knowledge base with new information or refined content.
