# AI-powered UGC Video Generation

## Overview
Generating user-generated content (UGC) style videos using artificial intelligence, including selecting avatars, voices, and customizing scripts based on product details.

**Use case:** Creating marketing ads, product reviews, or social media content at scale with AI-generated presenters.

## Key steps
1. Utilize product links to auto-fill details for efficiency.
2. Experiment with different video types (reaction, product in hand) to see what converts best.
3. Customize scripts to highlight key product features and calls to action.
4. Choose avatars and voices that align with your brand's target audience and tone.
