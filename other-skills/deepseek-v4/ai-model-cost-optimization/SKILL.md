# AI Model Cost Optimization

## Overview
Strategies for reducing the operational costs associated with using large language models, often by selecting more cost-effective alternatives without significant performance loss.

**Use case:** Reducing monthly AI subscription costs from hundreds to single digits by switching from Anthropic's Claude Opus to DeepSeek V4.

## Key steps
1. Compare pricing models (per token, per request) across different AI providers.
2. Benchmark alternative models against current solutions for performance and cost-effectiveness.
3. Leverage open-source or more competitively priced models that offer comparable capabilities.
