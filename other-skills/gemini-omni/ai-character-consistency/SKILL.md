# AI Character Consistency

## Overview
Maintaining the visual identity and appearance of a character across multiple scenes, outfits, and poses within an AI-generated video.

**Use case:** Ensuring a consistent protagonist in AI-generated stories or personalized video content.

## Key steps
1. Provide a diverse set of reference images showing the character from different angles and expressions.
2. Ensure reference images are high quality and clearly depict the character's features.
