# Gemini Omni Precision Video Editing

## Overview
Gemini Omni is Google's video-to-video model designed for extreme precision editing. Unlike earlier AI video tools where every generation randomly altered elements you wanted to keep, Omni excels at holding unchanged elements perfectly consistent while applying targeted edits.

## Key Techniques
- **Subtle lighting adjustment**: Change lighting conditions (warmer, cooler, add accent spotlights) while keeping subjects and backgrounds intact
- **Background swap**: Replace or modify background elements without affecting foreground subjects
- **Text overlay prep**: Add dark shading to specific regions to improve text legibility over video
- **Extreme creative edits**: Metallic skin effects, environment warping — but the real power is in the subtle stuff

## How to Apply
1. Open the Gemini app and access Gemini Omni
2. Upload your source video
3. Describe the specific change you want (be precise: "add a subtle warm accent light from the left side, keep everything else unchanged")
4. Review the output — Omni maintains element consistency far better than competing tools
5. Iterate with small, targeted prompts rather than sweeping changes

## Examples
- Adding a subtle dark shading to the lower third of a video for text overlay legibility
- Swapping a background from a neutral room to a more professional studio look
- Adding an accent spotlight from a specific direction to improve subject lighting
- Making subtle skin tone warmth adjustments for more cinematic color grading
