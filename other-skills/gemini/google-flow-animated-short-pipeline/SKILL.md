# AI Animated Short Film Pipeline via Google Flow

## Overview
A workflow for producing a full animated short film from a single text prompt using Google
Flow (Google DeepMind's free AI filmmaking tool at labs.google/fx/tools/flow).

## Key Techniques
- Generate a full storyboard from one text prompt before animating anything.
- Define camera angles, tone, style, and setting per scene rather than leaving it generic.
- Check character consistency across generated scenes before committing to a full render.
- Animate frame-by-frame with that consistency maintained through to the final render.

## How to Apply
1. Open Google Flow and describe the film with a single text prompt.
2. Review the generated storyboard; adjust camera angles, tone, style, and setting per scene.
3. Verify character consistency across scenes.
4. Animate the frame-by-frame scenes and render the final short.
