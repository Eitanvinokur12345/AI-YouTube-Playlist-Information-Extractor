# Iterative UI Design with Google Stitch

## Overview
Describe an app idea in plain English and Google Stitch generates the full UI — components, layout, and structure — in seconds. Iterate in real time with natural language commands and upload screenshots of existing apps to remix their design. Connects to Google Firebase for backend deployment.

## Key Techniques
- Upload a screenshot of any existing app to let Stitch analyze and remix its design.
- Use iterative plain-language commands (make button red, add a column) to refine in real time.
- Connect directly to Google Firebase from within Stitch for deployable backend alongside generated UI.

## How to Apply
Rapidly prototyping and iterating on app UIs without writing front-end code, using plain-language instructions and screenshot-to-design remixing.
