# Risk-taking

## Overview
The willingness to embrace uncertainty and potential negative outcomes for the chance of greater rewards, especially in adopting new technologies.

**Use case:** Making strategic decisions in an evolving AI landscape, where inaction can be more detrimental than calculated risks.

## Key steps
1. Focus on the long-term benefits of AI adoption, even if there are short-term costs or uncertainties.
2. Understand that the ability to take calculated risks will remain a valuable human trait.
