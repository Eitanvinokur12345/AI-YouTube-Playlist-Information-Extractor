# Learning from human demonstration (robotics)

## Overview
A method of training robots where human movements are captured (e.g., via sensor gloves) and directly transferred to the robot, allowing it to learn tasks without explicit programming.

**Use case:** Rapidly teaching robots new tasks, improving adaptability to varied environments and objects.
