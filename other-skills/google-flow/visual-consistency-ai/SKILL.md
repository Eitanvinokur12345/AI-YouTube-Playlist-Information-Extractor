# Visual Consistency in AI Generation

## Overview
Ensuring uniformity in character appearance, lighting, and framing across multiple AI-generated visual assets or video clips.

**Use case:** Professionalizing AI-generated content, maintaining brand identity, and improving viewer experience.

## Key steps
1. Utilize 'ingredient' features in AI tools to lock in character consistency.
2. Experiment with lighting parameters to match the desired mood and environment.
3. Standardize framing and camera angles for a cohesive look.
