# AI Short Drama Generation Workflow

## Overview
Higgsfield AI Supercomputer enables short drama production at scale through unified memory (persistent character identity) and prompt iteration for dialogue scenes. The workflow covers system migration, character setup, and scene-by-scene refinement to produce cinematic AI video output consistently.

## Key Techniques
- **Unified memory** — characters remain visually and behaviorally consistent across all generated clips
- **Prompt iteration** — refine specific scene prompts (dialogue, action, tone) until the output matches creative intent
- **System migration** — move between scene/character configurations to scale from one drama concept to many

## How to Apply
1. Open Higgsfield Supercomputer and define your character profiles (appearance, voice, persona).
2. Build scene prompts that describe the dialogue exchange, setting, and camera framing.
3. Generate a test clip; review character consistency and scene quality.
4. Iterate on the prompt — adjust dialogue phrasing, emotional tone, or action direction.
5. When satisfied, migrate your system config to produce the full drama sequence.

## Examples
- A short social media drama in 5 clips: one character setup → 5 iterated scene prompts → 5 consistent clips ready to post.
