# AI Business Idea Validation

## Overview
The ability to identify and validate profitable business ideas, particularly in the AI space, by analyzing market trends, community discussions, and problem-solution fit.

**Use case:** Starting a profitable AI startup or micro-SaaS.

## Key steps
1. Utilize AI agents to scrape the internet for emerging needs and trends.
2. Analyze community signals from platforms like Reddit, Facebook, and YouTube to gauge interest and pain points.
3. Evaluate ideas based on opportunity, problem severity, feasibility, and market timing.
