# Mood Board Creation & Application

## Overview
Creating a collection of images that define a specific aesthetic or 'mood', which the AI then analyzes to generate new images consistent with that style, including identifying keywords and negative prompts.

**Use case:** Establishing a consistent visual style across multiple generated images or exploring new styles based on curated examples.

## Key steps
1. Upload a batch of images with a similar style to create a mood board.
2. Review the AI-generated taste profile, keywords, and 'what to avoid' suggestions to understand the extracted style.
