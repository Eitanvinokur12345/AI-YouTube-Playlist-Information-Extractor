# Style Transfer

## Overview
Applying the style of one or more input images to a newly generated image, with control over the strength of the transfer.

**Use case:** Stylizing generated images to match a specific aesthetic or artistic influence.

## Key steps
1. Use a slider to control the intensity of the style transfer.
2. Combine multiple style images and adjust individual weightings for a blended style.
