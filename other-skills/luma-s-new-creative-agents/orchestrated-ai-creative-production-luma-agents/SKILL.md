# Orchestrated AI Creative Production with Luma Agents

## Overview
A workflow utilizing Luma's creative agents to generate a complete suite of creative production assets from a single text prompt. This involves parallel execution of sub-agents for tasks like scriptwriting, shot listing, voice-over generation, music and sound effects creation, brand identity development, storyboarding, and video production for keyframes, including an automated review process for error correction.

**Use case:** Rapid generation of comprehensive ad campaigns and creative content, particularly for video production, with integrated asset creation and quality control.
