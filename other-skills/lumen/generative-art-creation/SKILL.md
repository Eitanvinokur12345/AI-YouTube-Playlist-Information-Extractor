# Generative Art Creation

## Overview
The ability to create abstract visual art using generative algorithms and tools, producing unique and often looping animations or static images.

**Use case:** Creating dynamic backgrounds for videos, website headers, album covers, or digital art installations.

## Key steps
1. Experiment with different art modes and randomization to discover unique styles.
2. Adjust color palettes and individual parameters (hue, saturation, exposure, zoom, warp, etc.) to fine-tune the aesthetic.
3. Utilize the 'New style' feature to explore novel generative combinations beyond the base modes.
4. Save preferred styles and gradient sets for consistent branding or future use.
