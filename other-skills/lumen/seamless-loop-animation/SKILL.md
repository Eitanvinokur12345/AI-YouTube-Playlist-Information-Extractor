# Seamless Loop Animation

## Overview
The technique of creating animations that can repeat indefinitely without a noticeable break or jump, crucial for backgrounds and continuous visual effects.

**Use case:** Producing looping video backgrounds, animated GIFs for social media, or continuous visual elements for interactive experiences.

## Key steps
1. Ensure the generative parameters are set to produce a mathematically perfect loop.
2. Test the exported video/GIF to confirm the seamless transition at the loop point.
3. Consider the duration of the loop (e.g., 1-8 loops or 5-60 seconds) based on the intended use case.
