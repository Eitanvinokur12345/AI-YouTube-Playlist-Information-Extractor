# 3D Medical Image Reconstruction from Ultrasonic Data

## Overview
Processing vast amounts of raw ultrasonic echo data (up to 805 terabytes) from thousands of sensors with 2 petaflops of compute power to reconstruct hundreds of 2D slices into a full 3D map of internal organs, accurate to half a millimeter.

**Use case:** Medical diagnostics, health monitoring, advanced anatomical visualization.

## Details
- **Category:** image creation
- **Tool:** Midjourney Scanner  ·  **Quality:** 5/10
