# Ultrasonic Body Scanning

## Overview
The technique of using thousands of tiny transducers to emit ultrasonic waves into the body and listen for echoes, resolving motions smaller than an atom's width to create highly detailed internal body maps.

**Use case:** Medical diagnostics, preventative health, detailed anatomical mapping.
