# Strategic Decision Making (Human-in-the-Loop)

## Overview
Focusing human effort on high-level strategic decisions, judgment calls, and defining the next steps for AI agents, rather than repetitive execution.

**Use case:** Leveraging AI for 80% of execution while retaining human control over critical strategic choices, architecture, and judgment.

## Key steps
1. Delegate rhythmic, repetitive tasks to AI.
2. Concentrate on tasks requiring human intuition, empathy, and complex problem-solving.
