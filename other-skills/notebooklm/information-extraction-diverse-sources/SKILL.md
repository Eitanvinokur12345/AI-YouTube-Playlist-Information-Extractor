# Information Extraction from Diverse Sources

## Overview
Ability to extract specific information and answer questions by processing multiple documents in various formats (PDFs, spreadsheets, video/audio transcripts) simultaneously.

**Use case:** Quickly finding answers across different types of company documents like HR policies, financial reports, or training materials.

## Key steps
1. Ensure all relevant source documents are uploaded to NotebookLM.
2. Formulate clear and concise questions to get precise answers.
3. Verify the grounded answers by checking the cited sources.
