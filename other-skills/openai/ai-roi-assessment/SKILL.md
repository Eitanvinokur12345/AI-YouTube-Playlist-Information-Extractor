# AI ROI Assessment

## Overview
A methodology for evaluating the return on investment of artificial intelligence initiatives, focusing on real work done and cost per successful task rather than just per-token pricing.

**Use case:** Determining the economic value and effectiveness of AI investments within a company.

## Key steps
1. Focus on cost-per-outcome (cost per task) rather than cost-per-token.
2. Assess if the AI's work is real and dependable, or if it requires constant rechecking.
3. Evaluate if the AI's efficiency improves with scale.
