# Adversarial Dual-Prompt Technique

## Overview
This technique addresses AI sycophancy by deliberately prompting the model to first support your plan, then attack it in a separate prompt. The opposition response bypasses the model's reinforcement-learned tendency to please, surfacing genuine risks.

## Key Techniques
- Run two separate prompts: one asking the AI to defend your plan, one asking it to attack it
- Use explicit framing: "Now argue why this will fail" or "Be my devil's advocate"
- Compare both responses to identify blind spots and real weaknesses

## How to Apply
1. Present your plan or idea to the AI and ask for an honest assessment.
2. In a second prompt (or new session), explicitly ask: "Argue the strongest possible case for why this will fail."
3. Compare the two assessments side by side.
4. Use the attack points to stress-test your plan before committing.
5. Optionally ask for a third synthesis: "Given both perspectives, what should I change?"

## Examples
- Before launching a product, asking Claude to defend the go-to-market strategy, then asking it to argue why it will fail.
- Using ChatGPT to evaluate a business partnership, then asking for the strongest case against proceeding.
