# AI-Driven Investment Scoring Framework

## Overview
This framework uses AI to analyze a publicly traded company and produce a numeric investment score (1–100) by evaluating three dimensions: valuation (P/E ratios, market cap relative to peers), financial health (balance sheet, cash position), and growth metrics (revenue growth, market opportunity). NVIDIA scored 76/100 using this method.

## Key Techniques
- Define the three scoring pillars: valuation, financial health, growth — and ask AI to rate each sub-component
- Request a single composite 1–100 score with a breakdown by pillar
- Cross-check AI claims against authoritative financial sources (10-K, earnings reports)

## How to Apply
1. Identify the stock you want to analyze.
2. Prompt AI: "Score [COMPANY] as an investment from 1–100, evaluating: (1) valuation (P/E, PEG, market cap vs. peers), (2) financial health (cash, debt, FCF), (3) growth (revenue growth, TAM, competitive moat). Provide sub-scores and a composite score."
3. Review the breakdown — look for red flags in any single pillar.
4. Repeat for comparable companies to benchmark the score.

## Examples
- NVIDIA scored 76/100: strong growth and financial health (leads at ~$5T market cap, $41B balance sheet) offset by high P/E reflecting rich valuation.
