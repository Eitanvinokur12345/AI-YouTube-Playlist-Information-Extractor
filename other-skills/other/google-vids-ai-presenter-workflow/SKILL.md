# Google Vids AI Presenter: Free Avatar Video from Slides

## Overview
Upload a Google Slides deck to Google Vids (vids.new) to automatically generate a presenter video with an AI avatar reading the script. Choose from 53 avatars or build a custom avatar with Nano Banana 2; videos support 24 languages and have a free tier of 10 generations per month.

## Key Techniques
- Go to vids.new, upload a Google Slides deck, and Google Vids generates a presenter video with an AI avatar — free tier: 10 videos/month
- Use Nano Banana 2 to build a custom AI avatar for Google Vids presentations

## How to Apply
Creating professional presenter videos from existing slide decks without recording, using a free AI avatar that speaks in multiple languages.

## Examples
A polished presenter video with an AI avatar voice and face synced to the slide content, deployable in 24 languages.
