# Viral Video Preset Replication with Higgsfield AI

## Overview
Higgsfield AI's viral presets feature lets you instantly replicate the visual style of viral videos using pre-built templates. You select a preset and apply it to your own footage to produce trend-aligned content without manual editing.

## Key Techniques
- Browse Higgsfield's viral preset library for templates matching your desired style
- Apply a preset to your video footage with one click
- Iterate quickly by testing multiple presets against the same source footage

## How to Apply
1. Open Higgsfield AI and navigate to the Viral Presets section
2. Browse available presets and select one matching your target viral style
3. Upload or select your source video
4. Apply the preset and export the result

## Examples
- Short-form social media clips styled to match trending TikTok or Instagram Reels formats
- Product demo videos restyled to match viral unboxing or reaction video formats
