# Automated Lead Generation and Outreach with Manus AI

## Overview
Use Manus AI's Web Research feature to scrape targeted lead lists from LinkedIn and directories, then chain a second prompt to draft personalized 3-sentence outreach emails per lead and send via connected Gmail.

## Key Techniques
- Connect the Gmail connector so Manus can send emails directly from your inbox.
- Test with 10 sends first, measure reply rate, then scale.
- Personalize by instructing Manus to reference something specific from the lead's LinkedIn.

## How to Apply
Building a targeted list of 200 qualified leads with personalized email drafts and sending the first email.
