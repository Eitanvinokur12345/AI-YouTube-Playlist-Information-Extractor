# Chief of Staff Calendar Audit via Manus AI

## Overview
Schedule a recurring Manus AI agent that pulls Google Calendar, cross-references events against a goals document to evaluate goal alignment and time allocation, then audits for quality issues and posts a weekly Slack-formatted report with completeness score and top-5 priority action list.

## Key Techniques
- Attach a goals document to the agent so it cross-references events against actual annual goals.
- Schedule the agent for Saturday 5 PM so you receive the audit before the new week begins.
- Track your completeness score weekly to gamify better calendar hygiene.

## How to Apply
Automated weekly calendar audit ensuring time allocation matches high-leverage, goal-aligned work.

## Examples
From the source video: Weekly Slack-formatted report with goal alignment scores, time-split breakdown, calendar quality issues, and top-5 action list — posted automatically every Saturday.

## Compatibility
Manus AI (any)
