# Automated Competitor Intelligence with Manus AI

## Overview
Use Manus AI's Web Research feature to scrape and compare multiple competitors simultaneously, pulling pricing, positioning, customer reviews, social strategy, and ad campaigns, then compile findings into a comparison spreadsheet with differentiation opportunities.

## Key Techniques
- Chain the research step into a presentation step in the same conversation.
- Include ad library scanning in the prompt for more complete competitive intelligence.
- Keep a standing competitors list project to re-run monthly.

## How to Apply
Running a comprehensive competitor analysis that surfaces actionable differentiation in 30 minutes instead of 20 hours.

## Examples
From the source video: Comparison spreadsheet plus slide deck highlighting top differentiation opportunities, delivered to Slack.

## Compatibility
Manus AI (any)
