# CRM Dead-Deal Revival via Manus AI

## Overview
Connect Manus AI to a CRM or deals spreadsheet to automatically find stalled opportunities, analyze each deal's last touchpoint, then draft a personalized re-engagement email referencing the previous conversation AND including a relevant third-party article addressing the prospect's stated pain.

## Key Techniques
- Never send 'just checking in' — always instruct Manus to find a relevant article about the prospect's pain point.
- Set a recurring monthly calendar block to trigger the dead-deal review.
- Instruct Manus to analyze why the deal likely went cold based on the last touchpoint.

## How to Apply
Recovering stalled or ghosted sales deals by sending timely, personalized re-engagement emails that add value.
