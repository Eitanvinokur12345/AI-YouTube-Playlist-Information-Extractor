# Outlier-Based Content Calendar with Manus AI

## Overview
Prompt Manus AI to research top creators in your niche, identify posts that significantly outperform their average (outliers), and generate a 30-day content calendar with hooks, captions, CTAs, and optimal posting times based on those patterns.

## Key Techniques
- Instruct Manus to find 'outliers' — posts that significantly outperform the creator's average.
- Specify target audience, platforms, and content pillars in the prompt.
- Use monthly to batch content planning and reduce decision fatigue.

## How to Apply
Generating a validated 30-day social media content calendar from proven high-performing content patterns.
