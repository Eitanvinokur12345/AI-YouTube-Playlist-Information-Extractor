# AI-Driven Proactive Recruiting with Manus AI

## Overview
Use Manus AI to generate a hiring brief form, fill it out, then have Manus autonomously search LinkedIn and Indeed, rank candidates by fit score, and draft personalized outreach emails.

## Key Techniques
- Start by asking Manus to create an intake form for the role — it will ask the right qualifying questions.
- Have Manus rank candidates by fit score so you review strongest matches first.
- Include deal-breakers explicitly in the prompt.

## How to Apply
Finding and reaching top candidates for an open role without waiting for applications.
