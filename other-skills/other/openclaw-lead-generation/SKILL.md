# OpenClaw Satellite-Based B2B Lead Generation

## Overview
AI agent workflow that scrapes satellite imagery to identify high-value target properties by lot size, sun exposure, and recent ownership change; pulls homeowner contact data from public records; executes direct mail and digital follow-up outreach; and closes deals via automated pipeline management.

## Key Techniques
- Filter by recent ownership change — new homeowners are most likely to invest in upgrades.
- Combine satellite lot size + sun exposure filtering to pre-qualify for relevant service offering (pools need space, solar needs exposure).
- Pull directly from public records to own the lead data rather than paying for purchased lists.

## How to Apply
Generating qualified leads for home service businesses (pools, landscaping, solar) using satellite data rather than purchasing lead lists.

## Examples
From the source video: Automated pipeline of qualified, high-intent leads for $500K-$1.2M properties with outreach sequences and deal tracking.
