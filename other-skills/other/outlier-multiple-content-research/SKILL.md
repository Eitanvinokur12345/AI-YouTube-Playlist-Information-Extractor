# Outlier-Multiple Content Research

## Overview
A content-research technique that replaces "what's viral" with "what's an outlier": it measures
a post's performance relative to its own creator's baseline, so a small account's breakout post
counts as a real signal just as much as a big account's hit.

## Key Techniques
- Compute the outlier multiple: a post's views divided by the median views of that same
  creator's last 20-30 posts.
- Treat 3x (or higher) as a genuine signal, regardless of the account's absolute size.
- Search for outliers across multiple platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X), not just one.
- Pull the transcript of an outlier post for free, then rebuild its underlying structure in your
  own voice/words rather than copying sentences.

## How to Apply
1. For a creator/niche you're researching, gather view counts for their last 20-30 posts.
2. Compute the median, then divide each post's views by that median to get its outlier multiple.
3. Flag posts at 3x or higher as structurally worth studying, across all the platforms that
   creator posts to.
4. Pull the outlier post's transcript, extract its structure (hook, arc, CTA), and rewrite that
   structure in your own voice for your own content.
