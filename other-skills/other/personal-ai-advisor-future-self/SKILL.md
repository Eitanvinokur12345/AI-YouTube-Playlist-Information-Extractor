# Personal AI Advisor Persona (Future-Self Coaching)

## Overview
A custom AI persona built as a replica of the person you want to become, consulted daily as
an advisor so day-to-day decisions stay aligned with a long-term trajectory instead of being
made from scratch each time.

## Key Techniques
- Write the persona around a specific target identity (values, priorities, decision style),
  not a generic "life coach" system prompt.
- Consult it on live, in-the-moment decisions rather than only for occasional big-picture
  planning sessions.
- Treat it as reducing decision fatigue: the persona's consistent framework removes the need
  to re-derive your priorities every time a choice comes up.

## How to Apply
1. Write a detailed persona prompt describing the person you want to become: their values,
   priorities, and how they'd approach decisions.
2. Save it as a reusable system prompt / custom persona in your AI tool of choice.
3. Consult it daily on real decisions as they come up, not just for retrospective planning.
4. Refine the persona over time as your target identity becomes clearer.

## Examples
The source video frames this as an "ultimate AI cheat code": rather than building a generic
business assistant, the persona is an exact replica of the future self the user wants to
become, consulted every day to eliminate short-term decision fatigue.
