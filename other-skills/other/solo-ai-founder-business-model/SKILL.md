# Solo AI Founder Business Model Framework

## Overview
A 4-step framework for building profitable solo AI businesses, grounded in verified examples (Base44 $80M exit, Pieter Levels $3.5M/year, Danny Postma $3.6M/year). AI reduces the execution cost so one person with $200-1000/month in tools can do what previously required 15 people and Series A funding.

## Key Techniques
- **Step 1 — Find latent demand**: Mine Reddit/forums/review sites for the same complaint repeated by 100+ different people
- **Step 2 — Ship thin and fast**: Get to a paying v1 in weeks, not months — paying customers give better feedback than free users
- **Step 3 — Price on outcomes**: Charge 30-50% of what the customer currently spends on the process you automate; outcome-based pricing beats access pricing
- **Step 4 — Build distribution before launch**: Share everything in public (revenue, failures, process) for 6+ months to build an audience before you need it

## How to Apply
1. Go to Reddit, forums, and review sites — search for recurring complaints (look for 100+ different people saying the same thing)
2. Identify the workflow people are solving with spreadsheets + 3 tools + manual effort
3. Build the thinnest possible version someone will pay for — target 1 week of work, not 6 months
4. Price it: what does the customer spend today on this problem? Charge 30-50% of that as a monthly SaaS subscription
5. Start posting weekly about your build: revenue numbers, user count, failures, thinking — build the audience as you build the product
6. At $3M+ ARR, expect to add 2-5 people for customer success and operations — this is normal, not a failure of the model

## Examples
- **Base44**: Marshall Lomo built an AI app builder solo, 300k users in 3 months, $1M ARR in 6 months, sold to Wix for $80M
- **Photo AI**: Pieter Levels launched to 350k Twitter followers, $5,400 revenue week 1
- **Headshot Pro**: Danny Postma, solo from Bali, $3.6M ARR
