# Perplexity Computer Workflow Automation

## Overview
Perplexity Computer offers five core automated workflow templates that each triple output by removing manual multi-step execution. The agentic system browses, compiles, and delivers complete business deliverables autonomously.

## Key Techniques
- Use workflow templates for five core tasks: landing pages, competitor intelligence, sales briefs, slides, contracts
- Chain workflows in sequence to build a complete sales pipeline in one session
- Provide clear input data (company info, competitor names, contract text) to maximize output quality

## How to Apply
1. **Landing Page:** Provide your product description; Perplexity builds the page copy and structure.
2. **Competitor Intelligence:** Provide 3–5 competitor names; it gathers and synthesizes their positioning.
3. **Sales Brief:** Provide prospect info; it generates a customized brief.
4. **Slides:** Provide content outline; it creates a structured slide deck.
5. **Contract Review:** Paste contract text; it highlights key clauses and risks.

## Examples
- Automating weekly competitor analysis by running the intelligence workflow and exporting the report directly.
- Creating a sales brief for a new prospect in 2 minutes instead of 30 using the sales brief template.
