# Text-to-Voice with Descriptive Prompts

## Overview
Generating new voices by describing their characteristics (e.g., gender, age, tone, emotion) in natural language.

**Use case:** Creating unique character voices for narratives, games, or virtual assistants without needing existing audio samples.

## Key steps
1. Use plain English to describe desired voice characteristics.
2. Specify tone and emotion to match character requirements.
