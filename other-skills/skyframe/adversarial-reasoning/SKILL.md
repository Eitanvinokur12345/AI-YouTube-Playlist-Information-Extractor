# Adversarial Reasoning

## Overview
A technique where multiple AI agents debate a topic, with roles like architect (thesis), skeptic (antithesis), and an incorruptible judge (synthesis), to produce more accurate and less hallucinatory results by identifying and correcting flaws in each other's arguments.
