# Data Forecasting

## Overview
The process of making predictions about future events or trends based on historical data. This includes predicting sales, website traffic, revenue, and customer growth.

**Use case:** Predicting future business metrics, optimizing resource allocation, strategic planning.

## Key steps
1. Utilize pre-trained foundation models like TimesFM for zero-shot forecasting without extensive model training.
2. Feed historical time-series data to the model to generate instant predictions.
