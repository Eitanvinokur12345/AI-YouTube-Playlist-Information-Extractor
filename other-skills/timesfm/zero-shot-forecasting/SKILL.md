# Zero-Shot Forecasting

## Overview
The ability of a model to make accurate predictions on data it has never explicitly seen during training, by leveraging knowledge gained from a vast pre-training corpus.

**Use case:** Rapid forecasting for diverse business scenarios, quick insights without custom model development.

## Key steps
1. Use models pre-trained on large datasets of real-world time-points to achieve high accuracy on unseen data.
2. Reduces the need for domain-specific data scientists and lengthy training processes.
