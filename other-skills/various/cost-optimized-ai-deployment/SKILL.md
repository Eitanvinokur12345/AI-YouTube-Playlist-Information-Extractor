# Cost-Optimized AI Deployment

## Overview
Strategically allocating AI tasks to models based on their cost and capability, using cheaper models for routine 'grunt work' and reserving more expensive, powerful models for tasks where their superior judgment is critical and changes the outcome.

**Use case:** Reducing operational costs of AI-powered systems while maintaining high performance for critical tasks.

## Key steps
1. Use models like GLM 5.2 for reading emails, chats, documents, and creating initial briefings.
2. Avoid using expensive models for simple tasks like raw scanning, first-pass summaries, logs, formatting, or simple edits.
