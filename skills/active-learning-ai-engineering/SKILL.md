# Active Learning in AI Engineering

## Overview
A learning methodology for AI engineering that advocates for spending 80% of time actively writing code, building projects, and making mistakes, and only 20% of time consuming tutorials or learning content. This approach helps avoid 'tutorial hell' where one feels like they are learning but cannot apply the knowledge independently, especially critical in the fast-evolving field of AI.

**Use case:** Effective learning and development in AI engineering and other rapidly changing technical fields.

## Key steps
1. After watching a tutorial, immediately try to replicate the concepts or build something similar yourself.
2. Focus on hands-on practice and problem-solving to solidify understanding.
3. Embrace making mistakes as a crucial part of the learning process.
