# Advanced Prompt Engineering (Role-Playing/Multi-Persona Simulation)

## Overview
Designing prompts that instruct an AI to adopt multiple distinct personas or roles, each with a specific viewpoint or objective, to generate diverse and often conflicting analyses of a given problem or decision.

**Use case:** Gaining comprehensive insights, identifying potential pitfalls, exploring various strategies, and stress-testing ideas by simulating a debate or council of experts within the AI.

## Key steps
1. Clearly define each persona's characteristics, biases, and areas of focus (e.g., 'a skeptic hunting for what'll go wrong', 'a numbers person who only cares about ROI').
2. Specify the desired output format, such as individual arguments from each persona followed by a summary or verdict from a 'chairman' persona.
3. Provide sufficient context about the problem or decision to enable each persona to generate relevant and insightful arguments.
