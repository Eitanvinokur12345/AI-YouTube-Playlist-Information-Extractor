# Adversarial Multi-Agent Debate for Accuracy

## Overview
A method where multiple AI agents argue by proposing, critiquing, defending, and judging answers to improve overall accuracy and reduce hallucinations.

**Use case:** Improving the accuracy of AI-generated answers by leveraging debate and self-correction among multiple AI agents.

## Key steps
1. Use at least three agents with distinct roles: proposer, critic, and judge.
2. Allow agents to revise their answers mid-debate based on counterarguments.
3. Implement a judging mechanism to select the best final answer.
