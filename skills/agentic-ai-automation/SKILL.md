# Agentic AI Automation

## Overview
Setting up and scheduling AI agents to perform tasks autonomously based on predefined instructions and access to local resources.

**Use case:** Automating business workflows, such as product research, code review, or data analysis.

## Key steps
1. Define clear instructions for the AI agent.
2. Provide relevant resource documents as a knowledge base.
3. Schedule routines for regular execution.
4. Utilize 'Bypass permissions' for fully autonomous operation.
