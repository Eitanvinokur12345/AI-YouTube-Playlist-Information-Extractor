# Four Stages of AI Adoption Framework

## Overview
A progression model for AI adoption: Stage 1 (chatting/prompting) → Stage 2 (packaging into reusable skills) → Stage 3 (delegating tasks to AI workers) → Stage 4 (orchestrating AI systems). Most users are stuck at Stage 1; Stage 2 (skills) unlocks the real value.

## Key Techniques
- Move from Stage 1 (prompting) to Stage 2 (packaging) by defining a repeatable multi-step AI process for your most common task.
- A skill is a step-by-step AI process: define the input, the steps, and the expected output — run it the same way every time.
- Stage 4 (orchestration) = multiple AI workers running skills in parallel, each specialized.
