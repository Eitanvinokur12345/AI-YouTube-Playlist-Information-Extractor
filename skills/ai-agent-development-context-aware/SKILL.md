# AI Agent Development (Context-Aware)

## Overview
Developing AI agents that make decisions based on accumulated business context, including historical data, client preferences, and operational nuances.

**Use case:** Enabling AI to perform complex tasks like lead follow-up, reporting, and customer support with human-like understanding and decision-making.

## Key steps
1. Do not skip directly to agent development; ensure a robust data foundation first.
2. Design agents to learn from human approvals and rejections to adapt to specific client preferences.
