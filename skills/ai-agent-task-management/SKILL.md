# AI Agent-based Task Management

## Overview
The ability to leverage AI agents and sub-agents to break down complex problems, orchestrate tasks, perform research, analyze files, summarize information, and manage task lists in an organized manner.

**Use case:** Explaining codebases, writing articles/emails, general problem-solving, research, automation, multi-step processes.

## Key steps
1. Break down complex tasks into a structured to-do list.
2. Assign specialized AI sub-agents for specific research, digging, or testing sub-tasks.
3. Synthesize results from sub-agents and update the main task list.
4. Utilize orchestrating agents to manage the flow and progression of tasks.
