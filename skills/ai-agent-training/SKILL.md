# AI Agent Training

## Overview
The process of teaching an AI agent to perform specific tasks by providing feedback, corrections, and structured knowledge, similar to training a new human hire.

**Use case:** Onboarding and developing AI agents for specific job roles.

## Key steps
1. Treat the AI like a brand new employee, providing detailed feedback.
2. Document wins and corrections to build the AI's 'memory'.
3. Bundle repeating patterns of corrections and successful outputs into named skills.
