# AI Alternative Approaches Prompting

## Overview

**Use case:** Breaking out of tunnel vision when solving technical or creative problems
**Output:** A list of alternative approaches and solution paths to a given problem

## Key techniques & how to apply
1. Ask AI to suggest alternative approaches when you feel stuck in one solution path
