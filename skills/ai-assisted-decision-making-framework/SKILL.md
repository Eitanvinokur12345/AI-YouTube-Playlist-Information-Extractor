# AI-assisted decision making framework

## Overview
A structured approach to using AI to evaluate complex decisions by breaking them down into components, analyzing them from multiple angles, and synthesizing a comprehensive recommendation.

**Use case:** Business strategy, personal career choices, project planning, ethical dilemmas.

## Key steps
1. Clearly define the decision to be made.
2. Utilize specialized AI agents to provide diverse perspectives (e.g., risk, opportunity, execution).
3. Require the AI to identify areas of agreement and disagreement among its 'advisors' to highlight key considerations.
