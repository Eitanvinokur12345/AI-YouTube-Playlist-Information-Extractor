# Instruct AI to ask clarifying questions

## Overview
Include a directive in your prompts that encourages the AI to ask follow-up questions if it requires more information to provide a comprehensive or improved answer. This fosters an interactive process for refining outputs.

**Use case:** Enhancing the accuracy and completeness of AI responses through iterative refinement.

## Key steps
1. Append 'ask me any questions you need to give me a better answer' to your prompts.
