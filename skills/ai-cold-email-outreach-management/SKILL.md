# AI-powered Cold Email Outreach Management

## Overview
Leveraging AI to create, manage, and track cold email campaigns, including sequence generation, content drafting, personalization, performance analysis, and troubleshooting.

**Use case:** Automating and optimizing cold email outreach for product launches or lead generation.

## Key steps
1. Connect email platform (e.g., Saleshandy) to Claude via custom connector.
2. Use natural language prompts to define campaign goals, number of touchpoints, and duration.
3. Utilize AI for drafting email copy, subject lines, and incorporating personalization tags.
4. Ask AI to analyze campaign performance, identify underperforming sequences, and troubleshoot deliverability issues.
5. Integrate prospect management for quick additions to sequences.
