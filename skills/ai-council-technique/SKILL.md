# AI Council Technique

## Overview
A method for leveraging AI models by assigning them distinct personas (Contrarian, First Principles Thinker, Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Synthesizer) to critically debate and evaluate an idea from multiple perspectives, aiming to stress-test assumptions and identify weaknesses rather than seeking simple validation.

**Use case:** Idea validation, critical thinking, strategic planning, overcoming AI agreement bias, problem-solving, decision-making.

## Key steps
1. Define five distinct AI personas: Contrarian, First Principles Thinker, Devil's Advocate, Pragmatist, Synthesizer.
2. Instruct each persona to provide raw critique and challenge assumptions.
3. Encourage a structured debate among the personas.
4. Use the Synthesizer persona to consolidate feedback into a final, stress-tested conclusion.
