# AI Data Isolation

## Overview
The practice of ensuring that data from different clients or sources remains separate and inaccessible to others within a shared AI system, preventing data leakage and maintaining privacy.

**Use case:** Designing and implementing multi-tenant AI applications, particularly support bots or knowledge bases, to prevent cross-client data exposure.
