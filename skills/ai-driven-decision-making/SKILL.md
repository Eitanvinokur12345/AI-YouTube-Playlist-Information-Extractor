# AI-driven decision making

## Overview
Utilizing AI models to simulate multiple perspectives and arguments for a complex decision, allowing for a more robust evaluation of risks, opportunities, and second-order effects before committing resources.

**Use case:** Making strategic business decisions, evaluating partnerships, hiring, or any scenario requiring a multi-faceted analysis.

## Key steps
1. Craft a detailed prompt outlining the decision and the specific roles/personas the AI should adopt (e.g., skeptic, numbers person, growth person, outsider, doer).
2. Instruct the AI to argue each case individually before a 'chairman' weighs all perspectives and provides a final verdict and actionable first steps.
3. Ensure the prompt clearly defines the goals and constraints of the decision to guide the AI's analysis.
