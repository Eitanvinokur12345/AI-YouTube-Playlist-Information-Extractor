# AI Implementation Strategy

## Overview
The ability to strategically integrate AI solutions into business processes by first understanding existing workflows, identifying core problems, and assessing potential business value before deploying specific AI tools.

**Use case:** Ensuring successful and impactful AI adoption within an organization by aligning technology with business objectives.

## Key steps
1. Prioritize understanding current business processes and identifying specific pain points or opportunities for improvement.
2. Focus on the 'why' behind AI implementation, ensuring it addresses a real business problem or creates tangible value.
3. Avoid simply 'throwing tools' at problems without a clear strategy or understanding of their impact on the bottom line.
