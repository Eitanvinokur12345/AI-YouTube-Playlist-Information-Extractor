# AI Information Hierarchy

## Overview
The Information Hierarchy is a structured folder system that encodes all your business and personal context once. From that day forward, every AI tool you use — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini — immediately understands your context by loading the relevant folder. It compounds in value every day you use AI.

## Key Techniques
- Have Claude auto-organize your existing documents into a layered hierarchy
- Structure as: company overview → products/services → customers → processes → current projects
- Upload the relevant folder at the start of each new AI session

## How to Apply
1. Gather your key business documents (website copy, SOPs, client personas, product descriptions).
2. Tell Claude: "Organize all my documents into an AI-ready Information Hierarchy with clear sections."
3. Review the hierarchy and refine section names to match how you actually work.
4. Save as a local folder structure. When starting a new AI session, upload or paste the relevant sections.
5. Update the hierarchy monthly as your context evolves.

## Examples
- A marketing consultant uploads their "clients" and "current campaigns" folders when starting a content strategy session with any AI tool.
- A developer uploads their "tech stack" and "current sprint" folders when asking Claude for architecture advice.
