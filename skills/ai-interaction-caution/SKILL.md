# AI interaction caution

## Overview
Exercising caution when communicating sensitive or critical information to AI chatbots and when interpreting AI-generated responses, especially in fields requiring expert human judgment like law.

**Use case:** Avoiding misinterpretation or disclosure of sensitive information when using AI for preliminary research or advice.

## Key steps
1. Do not share confidential or personal information with public AI models.
2. Always verify AI-generated legal or medical advice with a qualified human professional.
3. Be aware of the limitations and potential biases of AI models.
