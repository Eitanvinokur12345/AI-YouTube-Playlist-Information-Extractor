# AI-powered Market Research

## Overview
Utilizing AI to analyze customer feedback, specifically low-rated reviews, to identify common complaints and unmet needs for existing products.

**Use case:** Generating new product ideas or improving existing ones by understanding customer pain points.

## Key steps
1. Focus on 1-star and 2-star reviews to pinpoint critical flaws.
2. Ask the AI to rank complaints by frequency for prioritization.
3. Use the identified complaints to brainstorm solutions for an improved product.
