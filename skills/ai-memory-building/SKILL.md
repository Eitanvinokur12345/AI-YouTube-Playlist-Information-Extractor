# AI Memory Building

## Overview
Creating structured projects within an AI assistant to store and recall information across different contexts, preventing the AI from 'forgetting' past interactions or data.

**Use case:** Maintaining persistent context for content creation, client management, operational tasks, and personal organization within an AI chatbot.

## Key steps
1. Create dedicated projects for different areas of your work/life (e.g., Content, Clients, Operations, Personal).
2. Store relevant information, documents, and past conversations within these projects.
