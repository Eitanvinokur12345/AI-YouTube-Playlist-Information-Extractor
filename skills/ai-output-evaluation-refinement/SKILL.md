# AI Output Evaluation & Refinement

## Overview
The ability to critically review and refine AI-generated content, recognizing that AI models like Claude lack specific brand context, proprietary data (e.g., Shopify data, return rates), and nuanced understanding of customer behavior. This skill involves identifying and correcting incorrect assumptions made by the AI to ensure the output aligns with business goals and brand voice.

**Use case:** Reviewing AI-generated marketing emails, product descriptions, or other business communications to ensure accuracy, brand consistency, and customer relevance.

## Key steps
1. You're still the one who reads the output and says, 'Uh, does that sound like us?' Or, 'Our customers will never respond to that.'
