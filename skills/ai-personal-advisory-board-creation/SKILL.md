# AI-Powered Personal Advisory Board Creation

## Overview
Leveraging AI to create a personalized advisory board. This involves feeding the AI personal strengths and weaknesses, identifying desired skills, and then creating AI personas based on real-world experts or an ideal future self to provide tailored advice and augment personal capabilities.

**Use case:** Personal development, strategic planning, skill gap analysis, mentorship, decision support.

## Key steps
1. Input your current perceived strengths and weaknesses into the AI.
2. Prompt the AI to identify skills necessary for your long-term goals that you currently lack.
3. Research real-world experts or historical figures who embody these desired skills.
4. Create detailed AI personality profiles for these individuals, custom augmented to your specific needs.
5. Develop an AI persona representing your ideal future self to offer long-term guidance and advice.
