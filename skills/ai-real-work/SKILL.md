# Using AI for real work

## Overview
Applying AI to solve real business problems, tasks, and generate tangible outputs, rather than just using it for novelty or experimentation.

**Use case:** Solving bottlenecks, automating processes, generating revenue.

## Key steps
1. Focus on real problems, not just cool outputs.
2. Identify bottlenecks in your business chain.
3. Prioritize tasks that buy back time, reduce mistakes, or multiply output.
