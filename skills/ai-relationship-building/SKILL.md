# AI Relationship Building (Personalization)

## Overview
Teaching an AI assistant about your personal goals, background, values, and preferred working/communication styles to tailor its responses and actions.

**Use case:** Customizing AI output to match personal tone, detail level, problem-solving approach, and specific professional context.

## Key steps
1. Create a 'YOU.md' file detailing your identity, background, goals, and values.
2. Create a 'HOW_TO_WORK.md' file outlining preferred writing tone, response structure, and problem-solving methods.
3. Upload these files to each relevant AI project.
