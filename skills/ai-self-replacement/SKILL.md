# Replacing yourself with AI (10-80-10 Rule)

## Overview
A strategic approach to automate personal work by breaking it into 10% ideation (human), 80% execution (AI), and 10% integration/refinement (human).

**Use case:** Automating personal tasks, increasing productivity, scaling individual output.

## Key steps
1. Record yourself doing the task (e.g., Zoom screen share).
2. Transcribe the recording.
3. Feed the transcript/recording to AI.
4. Iterate and refine AI's output until it can do 80% of the task.
5. Continuously train AI by feeding it more examples and feedback.
