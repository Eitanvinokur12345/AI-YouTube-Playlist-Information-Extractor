# AI Subscription Management

## Overview
Strategically managing AI tool subscriptions and usage limits to optimize cost and access to features, including downgrading or upgrading plans based on usage patterns.

**Use case:** Optimizing AI tool costs and ensuring continuous access to necessary AI capabilities without hitting usage limits.

## Key steps
1. Monitor usage of different AI models to identify which tasks consume the most resources.
2. Consider balancing usage across multiple AI tools if their strengths align with different task types.
3. Downgrade subscriptions for tools that are not being fully utilized to save costs.
4. Upgrade subscriptions only when hitting limits becomes a frequent issue for critical tasks.
