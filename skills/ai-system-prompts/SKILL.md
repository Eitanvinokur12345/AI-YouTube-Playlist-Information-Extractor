# Create and utilize AI system prompts

## Overview
After a successful interaction or a series of prompts that yield a desired result, ask the AI to synthesize those instructions into a 'system prompt.' This allows for the reuse of effective conversational context and instructions without repetitive explanations.

**Use case:** Standardizing AI behavior, streamlining workflows for recurring tasks, maintaining consistent output style.

## Key steps
1. When an AI conversation finally works, ask the AI to create a system prompt from it.
