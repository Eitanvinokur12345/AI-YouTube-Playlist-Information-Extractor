# AI Task Prioritization Before Automation

## Overview
A productivity strategy of identifying your three most repetitive tasks before adopting AI tools, then targeting automation at those specific pain points. This approach avoids tool-chasing and focuses AI energy on maximum ROI tasks.

**Use case:** Workflow planning before AI automation adoption
**Output:** Focused AI automation strategy targeting highest-impact repetitive tasks

## Key techniques & how to apply
1. List your three most repetitive tasks before evaluating any AI tool
2. Automate specific pain points rather than adopting AI for its own sake

## General tips
- Stop chasing every new AI tool — identify repetitive tasks first, then automate those specific pain points
