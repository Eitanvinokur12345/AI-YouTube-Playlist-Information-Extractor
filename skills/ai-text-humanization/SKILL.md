# AI Text Humanization

## Overview
A technique to rewrite AI-generated text to remove common 'signs of AI writing' and make it sound more human, while preserving meaning and facts. This is achieved by training an AI model on guidelines for identifying AI writing patterns.

**Use case:** Making AI-generated content indistinguishable from human-written content, avoiding AI detection, improving natural language flow.

## Key steps
1. Use a comprehensive guide like Wikipedia's 'Signs of AI writing' as training data.
2. Explicitly instruct the AI to preserve meaning and facts.
3. Instruct the AI not to add new information, only to rephrase existing content.
4. Review the output for 'overcorrection' to ensure it still sounds natural and competent.
