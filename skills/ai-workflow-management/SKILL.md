# AI Workflow Management

## Overview
The ability to structure and manage AI-driven tasks and data independently of specific AI models, focusing on owning the underlying data and processes.

**Use case:** Ensuring continuity and adaptability of AI operations despite changes in underlying AI models or tools.

## Key steps
1. Focus on creating a 'folder' of your files, rules, and skills.
2. Ensure your processes are model-agnostic and portable.
3. Integrate with existing business applications through a standardized connection layer.
