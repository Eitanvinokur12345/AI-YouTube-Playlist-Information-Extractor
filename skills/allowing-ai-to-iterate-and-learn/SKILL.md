# Allowing AI to iterate and learn

## Overview
Permitting the AI to 'struggle' and iterate on tasks without constant intervention, based on the understanding that it will learn and improve its performance over time from these experiences.

**Use case:** Fostering the AI's ability to self-correct and refine its output through iterative processes, leading to more robust and accurate results.

## Key steps
1. Don't 'babysit' the AI; allow it to loop and learn from its attempts to improve its performance over time.
