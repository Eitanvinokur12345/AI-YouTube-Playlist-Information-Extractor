# ATS Keyword Identification

## Overview
Leveraging AI to identify critical keywords that Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan for in resumes for specific job roles.

**Use case:** Resume tailoring, increasing interview chances

## Key steps
1. Upload your resume to the AI.
2. Ask the AI to act as a senior recruiter.
3. Prompt the AI to list job titles and the exact ATS keywords for each.
