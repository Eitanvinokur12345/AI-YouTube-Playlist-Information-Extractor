# Attention Mechanism

## Overview
A component in modern language models that allows the system to keep track of context across long conversations. It enables the model to look back at the entire conversation and weigh which earlier parts are most relevant to what it's currently generating, influencing the answer being written.

**Use case:** Enhancing conversational AI, improving context retention in Large Language Models (LLMs), maintaining coherence in multi-turn dialogues.
