# Brand and Design Consistency with AI

## Overview
Using AI to analyze an existing website's branding (colors, typography, layout) and then generating new content (like a pitch deck) that adheres to those established brand guidelines.

**Use case:** Ensuring all marketing and design materials are consistent with a company's brand identity.

## Key steps
1. Provide your website URL to the AI to generate a design document.
2. Use the generated design document as a reference for subsequent content creation tasks.
