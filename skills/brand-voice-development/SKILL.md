# Brand Voice Development

## Overview
Defining and implementing a consistent voice and tone for content to ensure it sounds authentic to the brand or individual.

**Use case:** Making AI-generated content sound like it was written by a specific founder, not generic AI slop.

## Key steps
1. Describe the voice using adjectives (e.g., direct, conversational, experience-driven, practical).
2. Provide examples of what the voice looks like in writing.
3. List specific words/phrases to NEVER use (AI-slop phrases).
4. List specific on-brand phrases, catchphrases, openers, and transitions.
5. Use direct address ('you') if it's part of the brand's style.
6. Pull examples from actual writing or speech rather than inventing them.
