# Building a story bank for AI-assisted storytelling

## Overview
Create a structured folder system to collect and organize story elements (screenshots, quotes, posts) by emotion, topic, and story type for AI retrieval and organization.

**Use case:** Organizing raw story material to enable AI to retrieve relevant stories based on specific themes or emotions.

## Key steps
1. Use consistent naming conventions for folders and files
2. Regularly prune and update the story bank to avoid clutter
3. Include metadata (e.g., emotion tags) for better AI retrieval
