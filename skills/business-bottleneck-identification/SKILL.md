# Identifying business bottlenecks

## Overview
Analyzing the business process chain (Attention, Leads, Sales, Delivery, Retention) to pinpoint the single biggest constraint hindering revenue or growth.

**Use case:** Finding revenue leaks, optimizing workflows, prioritizing AI deployment.

## Key steps
1. Walk through every stage of your business flow.
2. Ask: 'If I 10X this tomorrow, would my revenue actually go up or would I create another bottleneck?'
3. Focus on areas where leads or customers 'leak' out of the pipeline.
