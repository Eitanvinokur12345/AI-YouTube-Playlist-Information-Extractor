# Business Value Assessment for AI

## Overview
The capability to evaluate how AI solutions can generate measurable business value, such as increased efficiency, cost savings, or new revenue streams, before investment and deployment.

**Use case:** Making informed decisions about AI investments by ensuring that proposed solutions align with strategic business goals and offer a clear return on investment.

## Key steps
1. Clearly define the metrics by which the success of AI implementation will be measured.
2. Conduct thorough analyses of potential ROI and impact on key performance indicators (KPIs).
3. Engage stakeholders from various departments to ensure a holistic understanding of business needs and potential AI benefits.
