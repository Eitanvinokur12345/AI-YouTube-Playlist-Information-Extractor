# Challenging AI responses for alternatives

## Overview
A technique to improve AI interaction by not accepting the first answer and actively requesting alternative approaches or solutions to a given problem.

**Use case:** To generate a wider range of ideas, solutions, or perspectives from an AI, moving beyond its initial, potentially biased or limited, response.

## Key steps
1. Challenge the first answer provided by the AI.
2. Explicitly ask the AI to suggest alternative approaches or solutions.
3. Repeat this process every single time to encourage diverse outputs.
