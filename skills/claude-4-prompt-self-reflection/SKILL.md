# Claude 4-Prompt Self-Reflection Chain

## Overview
A structured four-prompt chain that uses Claude's cross-session memory to surface personality patterns, hidden fears, repeating behaviors and their root causes, then generates a personalized 30-day change plan. For self-reflection and personal development only — not a replacement for professional therapy.

## Key Techniques
- Sequence the four prompts progressively: surface → patterns → root cause → action plan
- Add "Be brutal. Don't soften it." to get genuinely useful rather than diplomatic feedback
- Prompt 4 must explicitly request a specific, time-bound plan to make output actionable

## How to Apply
1. Prompt 1: "Based on everything you know about me from our past conversations, tell me my personality type, my deepest insecurities, the fears I won't admit out loud, and what I actually want in life but I'm too scared to say."
2. Prompt 2: "Tell me the patterns I keep repeating that are quietly destroying my life. Be brutal. Don't soften it."
3. Prompt 3: "Act as my therapist. Based on everything you know, what is the one wound from my past that is still controlling my decisions today?"
4. Prompt 4: "Now reframe it. Give me the exact mindset shift I need and a 30-day plan to actually heal this. Be specific."

## Examples
- A founder uses the chain to identify why they keep self-sabotaging client relationships — and gets a 30-day relationship-repair plan.
- A professional uses it before a major career decision to surface unconscious fears affecting their judgment.

**Disclaimer**: This technique is for self-reflection only. If you are struggling, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional.
