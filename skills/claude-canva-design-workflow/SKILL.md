# Claude-Canva Design Workflow via Connector

## Overview
A workflow that connects Claude to Canva through the official Canva connector, enabling natural-language design generation and editing without leaving the Claude interface. Claude handles content generation while Canva provides the full visual editing layer.

## Key Techniques
- Connect Canva account via Claude → Customize → Connectors panel (one-time setup)
- Issue natural-language commands to create specific design types: "Create a 10-slide carousel about X"
- Switch to Canva for fine-grained customization (fonts, colors, branding) after AI generation

## How to Apply
1. In Claude, go to **Customize → Connectors → Browse Connectors → Canva** and connect your account.
2. Start a new conversation and specify your design: type (carousel/poster/banner), topic, target audience, and tone.
3. Claude generates the design directly in Canva (accessible from your Canva account).
4. Open the design in Canva for any visual adjustments.
5. Iterate by returning to Claude and requesting edits via natural language.

## Examples
- "Create a 5-slide Instagram carousel about AI tools for marketers with a modern blue color scheme."
- "Design a LinkedIn banner for a software agency with the tagline 'Ship Faster with AI'."
- "Make a promotional poster for a webinar on June 30, including date, time, and speaker info."
