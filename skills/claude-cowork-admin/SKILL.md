# Claude Cowork Admin Automation

## Overview
This skill covers setting up Claude Cowork on the desktop app and connecting it to Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar so it can read your inbox, draft contextual email replies, and manage calendar tasks. The result is a hands-off admin workflow that handles routine correspondence and scheduling.

## Key Techniques
- Install Claude Cowork desktop app and authenticate Google Workspace connectors
- Connect Gmail to allow Claude to read inbox and draft replies in your voice
- Connect Google Drive so Claude can reference documents when drafting responses
- Connect Google Calendar for schedule-aware email and task management

## How to Apply
1. Download and install the **Claude Cowork** desktop app.
2. Go to **Connectors** and connect **Gmail** — authorize access to your inbox.
3. Connect **Google Drive** — authorize access to your documents.
4. Connect **Google Calendar** — authorize access to your schedule.
5. Open a Claude Cowork chat and say: "Triage my inbox — flag urgent emails and draft replies to anything needing a response."
6. Review and send the drafted replies, making any edits needed.

## Examples
- "Summarize all unread emails from the last 24 hours and draft replies to anyone waiting on me."
- "Check my calendar and draft a schedule summary for tomorrow's meetings."
- "Find the contract in Drive for [client] and draft a follow-up email."
