# Design Motion Principles by Kyle Zen

## Overview
A Claude skill combining the UI/UX philosophies of three leading designers into one motion design framework. It analyzes your current design, identifies problems, and proposes or applies fixes.

**Use case:** Use to audit and improve existing Claude-generated UI designs — the skill diagnoses what's wrong and fixes it.

## Key steps
1. Pass your current design to the skill and ask for a motion audit before finalizing
2. The skill combines philosophies from multiple design experts into a single critique pass
