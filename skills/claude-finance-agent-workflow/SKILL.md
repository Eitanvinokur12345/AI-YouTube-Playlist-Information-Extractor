# Claude Finance Agent Workflow

## Overview
Workflow using Anthropic's 10 specialized finance AI agents built on Claude for comprehensive financial analysis. Covers market research (aggregating news and analyst ratings), stock valuation model building in Excel, and earnings call review to evaluate investment holding reasons.

**Use case:** Automating multi-step financial analysis including market research, earnings review, and stock modeling without expensive human analysts

## Key steps
1. Use the specialized market researcher agent to aggregate analyst ratings and news rather than doing it manually
2. Chain multiple finance agents: market research → model building → earnings review for end-to-end investment analysis
