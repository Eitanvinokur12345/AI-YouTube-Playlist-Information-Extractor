# Claude Legal Document Analysis Skills

## Overview
Anthropic launched three free legal AI skills inside Claude: Contract Reviewer, Compliance Checker, and Legal Risk Assessment. They analyze legal documents, flag risky clauses, generate redlines, and screen for privacy regulation issues — automating what previously required expensive manual review.

## Key Techniques
- Contract Reviewer: flags risky clauses, generates redlines, summarizes key terms
- Compliance Checker: screens for GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulation compliance issues
- Legal Risk Assessment: identifies liability exposure and flags high-risk provisions

## How to Apply
1. Open Claude and load the appropriate legal skill (Contract Reviewer, Compliance Checker, or Legal Risk Assessment) from Claude's Capabilities.
2. Upload or paste the legal document.
3. For contract review: ask "Review this contract, flag all risky clauses, and generate suggested redlines."
4. For compliance: ask "Check this document for GDPR and CCPA compliance issues."
5. For risk assessment: ask "Identify the top liability risks in this document."
6. Always have a licensed attorney review the output for any legally binding decision.

## Examples
- A founder reviews a SaaS vendor agreement with Contract Reviewer before signing, catching an unfavorable IP assignment clause.
- A small business uses Compliance Checker to audit their privacy policy before launching in Europe.
