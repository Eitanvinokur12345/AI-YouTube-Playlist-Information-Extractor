# Claude Pre-Mortem Project Risk Analysis

## Overview
The pre-mortem technique flips the normal planning mindset: instead of asking "how do we succeed?", you tell Claude "this project has already failed" and ask it to explain why. This reversal unlocks honest identification of hidden assumptions, weak links, and execution risks before resources are committed.

## Key Techniques
- **Imagined failure framing**: "This project launched 6 months ago and failed completely. What went wrong?"
- **Assumption surface**: Ask Claude to list every hidden assumption baked into the plan
- **Weak point audit**: Identify which dependencies, timelines, or skills are the most fragile
- **Risk prioritization**: Score identified risks by likelihood × impact

## How to Apply
1. Write out your project plan or idea in plain text
2. Send to Claude with this framing: "Imagine this project launched 6 months ago and failed completely. You are a post-mortem analyst. What went wrong? List all root causes."
3. Ask Claude to organize findings by: (a) hidden assumptions, (b) execution risks, (c) external dependencies
4. Ask for a top-5 risk prioritization by likelihood × impact
5. Use the findings to adjust the plan before committing

## Examples
- SaaS launch: Claude surfaces "the pricing assumption was never validated" and "the integration dependency on a third-party API has no fallback"
- Hiring plan: Claude identifies "relies on finding a niche skill in a small market" and "timeline assumes zero onboarding friction"
- Marketing campaign: Claude catches "assumes competitor won't respond" and "launch date conflicts with industry event"
