# Claude Projects Business Context Setup

## Overview
This skill covers the essential setup step most people skip when using Claude Projects: uploading your actual business documents and a writing style guide so Claude writes like you, not a generic template. The result is a project that consistently produces on-brand content without needing repeated context in every prompt.

## Key Techniques
- Upload real business documents (proposals, emails, reports) to establish writing context
- Include a writing style guide specifying tone, vocabulary, and format preferences
- Configure custom instructions at the project level for persistent behavior across sessions

## How to Apply
1. Open Claude and create or open a **Project**.
2. In the Project settings, upload your actual business documents (e.g., past proposals, client emails, brand guidelines).
3. Add a **writing style guide** — list preferred tone, vocabulary, sentence length, and any terms to avoid.
4. Set **custom instructions** to tell Claude your role, audience, and any standing preferences.
5. Test with a sample task to verify the output matches your voice before deploying widely.

## Examples
- A marketing consultant uploads their top 5 client decks + brand voice doc → Claude now writes pitch emails that sound exactly like them.
- A business owner adds their CRM notes and product descriptions → Claude drafts proposals using real product language.
