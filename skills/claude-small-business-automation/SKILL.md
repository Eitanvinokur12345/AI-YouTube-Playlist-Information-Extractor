# Claude Small Business Automation Pack

## Overview
Claude Cowork's free "For Small Business" plugin installs 33 AI skills and 12 connectors in one click. It handles payroll via QuickBooks, creates social media content via Canva, and automatically chases unpaid invoices — turning Claude into a full-stack small business operator.

## Key Techniques
- One-click install of the "For Small Business" pack from inside Claude Cowork
- QuickBooks connector for payroll handling
- Canva creator skill for social media content generation
- Automated invoice follow-up workflow

## How to Apply
1. Open Claude Cowork (cowork.claude.ai).
2. Go to the Skills & Connectors section.
3. Find the "For Small Business" pack and click Install.
4. Connect your QuickBooks account for payroll, and your Canva account for social content.
5. Start new chats using the installed skills: "Process this week's payroll" or "Create 5 social posts for my product."
6. Set up automated invoice reminders by configuring the invoice chase workflow.

## Examples
- A freelancer installs the pack and automates their monthly invoicing follow-up, recovering payment 3 days faster on average.
- A small retail shop uses the Canva skill to generate a week of social media posts in one 10-minute chat.
