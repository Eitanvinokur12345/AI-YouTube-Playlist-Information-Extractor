# Claude 5-Prompt Workday Automation Suite

## Overview
Five recurring Claude prompt templates designed to automate the most time-consuming parts of a knowledge worker's day. Together they can reduce active keyboard time from 8 hours to under an hour per day, clawing back 30+ hours per week without specialized tooling.

## Key Techniques
- Morning brief: pull Gmail, Slack, calendar, and news into one page (47 min → 4 min)
- Email triage: draft replies in your voice by feeding Claude your last 5 sent emails (90 min → 11 min)
- Meeting dossier: every past touchpoint plus 3 targeted questions to ask (30 min → 3 min)
- Weekly status report: pull from Linear, Notion, and Slack with a "won't fake metrics" guardrail (2 hrs → 7 min)
- Content repurposing: one post becomes a thread, LinkedIn post, newsletter section, and promotional email (90 min → 12 min)

## How to Apply
1. For each prompt, apply the Prompt Shape Formula: role in line one + locked format + hard stop.
2. Morning brief: connect Claude to Gmail, Slack, and calendar data (via API or manual paste) and run each morning.
3. Email triage: paste your last 5 sent emails first, then paste unread emails to triage.
4. Meeting dossier: pull past interaction notes and run before every client/stakeholder meeting.
5. Weekly report: paste data dumps from Linear, Notion, and Slack each Monday morning.
6. Content repurposing: paste one finished piece and get four distribution formats instantly.

## Examples
- Morning brief consolidates overnight Slack threads, urgent emails, 3 calendar blocks, and top AI news in 4 minutes.
- Email triage trained on 5 sent emails produces draft replies that sound like the author, not a generic AI.
- Weekly report with "Do NOT invent metrics" instruction produces honest reports even when data is incomplete.
