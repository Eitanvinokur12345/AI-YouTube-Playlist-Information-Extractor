# Contextual Prompt Engineering

## Overview
Crafting AI prompts that include detailed context such as persona, audience, specific goals, desired tone, and examples to generate highly relevant and tailored outputs, moving beyond simple, generic queries.

**Use case:** Generating personalized sales emails, marketing copy, or any business communication that needs to align with a specific brand voice and target audience.

## Key steps
1. Define the AI's persona (e.g., 'You are writing as Nick from Reprises AI').
2. Describe your business and its offerings.
3. Identify your target audience and their pain points.
4. Clearly state the desired output and its purpose.
5. Specify the tone and style (e.g., 'direct, slightly bold, simple, no fluff').
6. Provide one or more examples of desired output.
