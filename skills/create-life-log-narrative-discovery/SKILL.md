# Creating a life log for narrative discovery

## Overview
Maintain a folder of voice memos, photos, notes, and observations to capture life events that can later be connected by AI to form unexpected story narratives.

**Use case:** Transforming personal experiences into structured story material by identifying patterns across different media types.

## Key steps
1. Record voice memos immediately after significant events
2. Include timestamps and context in notes
3. Use AI to cross-reference unrelated entries for novel story angles
