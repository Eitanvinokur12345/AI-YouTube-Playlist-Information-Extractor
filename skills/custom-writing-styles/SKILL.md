# Custom Writing Styles

## Overview
Creating and applying specific writing styles within Claude by providing examples of desired tone, format, and voice.

**Use case:** Ensuring consistent brand voice, tone, or specific output formats for different types of content (e.g., emails, blog posts, social media).

## Key steps
1. Paste examples of your own writing (e.g., typical emails, LinkedIn posts) to train Claude.
2. Create multiple styles for different content types or audiences.
