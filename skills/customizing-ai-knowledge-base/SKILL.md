# Customizing AI Knowledge Base

## Overview
Uploading personal or proprietary files and documents into an AI project's knowledge base, allowing the AI to generate responses based on specific, real-world data and context rather than generic internet information.

**Use case:** Generating reports, emails, or notes in a specific style or based on internal company data.

## Key steps
1. Upload actual work files (e.g., last month's report, emails, style notes)
2. Maintain 'one project, one theme' to avoid dumping everything in one place
