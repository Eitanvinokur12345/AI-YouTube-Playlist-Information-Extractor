# Daily storytelling practice with AI feedback

## Overview
Use AI to generate random words and emotions, then practice telling a 60-second story connecting them. Submit the transcript to AI for analysis of attention retention and improvement suggestions.

**Use case:** Developing storytelling skills through rapid iteration and immediate feedback.

## Key steps
1. Start with simple word-emotion pairs
2. Record sessions to track progress
3. Focus on one feedback point per session
