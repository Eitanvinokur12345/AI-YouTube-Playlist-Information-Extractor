# Data Ingestion and Consolidation

## Overview
Pulling data from disparate systems (CRM, spreadsheets, booking apps, email inboxes, old databases) into a single, unified database.

**Use case:** Creating a central 'brain' for all business operations, essential before deploying AI agents to ensure they have complete context.

## Key steps
1. Prioritize data consolidation as the first step in any AI project.
2. Ensure the database is comprehensive and acts as the single source of truth.
