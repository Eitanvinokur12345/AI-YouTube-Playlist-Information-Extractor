# Defining AI Persona/Preferences

## Overview
Setting up specific instructions for the AI to adopt a certain role, tone, and response style, ensuring outputs align with user's professional context and communication needs.

**Use case:** Tailoring AI responses to specific professional roles (e.g., HR manager) and desired communication styles (e.g., concise, no jargon, steps not lectures).

## Key steps
1. Lead with who you are (e.g., 'I'm an HR manager at a 200-person company')
2. Specify desired reply style (e.g., 'Reply concise, no corporate jargon. Give me the steps, not lecture.')
3. Keep preferences under ~1500 characters
