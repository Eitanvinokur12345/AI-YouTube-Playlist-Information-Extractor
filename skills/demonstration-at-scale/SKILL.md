# Demonstration at scale

## Overview
Implementing methods to showcase expertise, product quality, or service effectiveness to a broad audience, often by integrating proof into core business operations.

**Use case:** Building trust and credibility with potential customers by visibly proving value, especially in industries where stakes are high.

## Key steps
1. Integrate proof-generating activities into customer service processes.
2. Document customer success stories and transformations.
3. For consumer products, physically demonstrate usage and results.
4. For services, walk through campaigns/processes with clients and showcase outcomes.
