# Digital Footprint Management

## Overview
Identifying and managing personal information exposed on the internet through data brokers and people search sites.

**Use case:** Privacy protection, personal data removal from public databases.

## Key steps
1. Use Claude to search for your personal information on data broker sites.
2. Request opt-out links and removal instructions from Claude.
3. Utilize Claude's Cowork feature to automate the submission of removal requests.
