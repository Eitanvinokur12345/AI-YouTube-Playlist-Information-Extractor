# Digital Footprint Removal Automation

## Overview
Using an AI assistant like Claude to identify data brokers and people search sites exposing personal information, and then automating the submission of removal requests to those sites.

**Use case:** Enhancing online privacy and security by deleting personal data from the internet.

## Key steps
1. Ensure to provide your full name and location for accurate search results.
2. Regularly check for new data brokers as information can reappear.
3. Verify the removal requests have been processed by checking the sites after some time.
