# DRAG Framework for AI-Augmented Thinking

## Overview
The DRAG framework is a four-step approach to using AI to sharpen your thinking rather than replace it: Delegate, Reflect, Augment, and Generate. It is designed to build human intelligence alongside AI rather than becoming dependent on it.

**Use case:** Personal productivity and skill development using AI as a cognitive augmentation tool
**Output:** Sharper thinking, faster decision-making, and retained human expertise alongside AI use

## Key techniques & how to apply
1. Use AI for intelligent laziness — delegate routine tasks so you can focus on higher-order thinking
2. Treat AI as a thinking gym: push back on its outputs to strengthen your own reasoning

## General tips
- Use AI to augment your decision-making rather than outsourcing your thinking entirely
