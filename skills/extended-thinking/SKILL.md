# Extended Thinking for Complex Tasks

## Overview
Activating 'Extended thinking' mode in Claude to encourage the AI to reason through complex problems step-by-step before generating a response, leading to more thorough and accurate answers.

**Use case:** Tackling intricate analytical tasks, strategic planning, or any problem requiring deep reasoning and multi-step processing.

## Key steps
1. Toggle on 'Extended thinking' when dealing with complex prompts that require detailed analysis or multi-step reasoning.
2. Be aware that this mode may take longer to generate a response but will provide a more comprehensive answer.
