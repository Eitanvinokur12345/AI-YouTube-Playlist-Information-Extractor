# Extreme close-up prompting

## Overview
Prompt for extreme close-up camera angles to capture intricate details, textures, and intimate perspectives in AI images.

**Use case:** Highlight fine details, textures, eyes, materials, or small objects with macro-level intimacy

## Key steps
1. Use 'extreme close-up', 'macro shot', or 'super macro' in prompts
2. Specify subject: eye, texture, fabric, mechanical parts
3. Add 'shallow depth of field' for professional bokeh
