# Focused AI Process Automation

## Overview
Identify the single business process that consumes the most time and automate only that process with AI to maximize ROI.

**Use case:** Businesses looking to implement AI efficiently by targeting the highest-impact process rather than broad AI adoption.

## Key steps
1. Analyze internal data to find the process bleeding the most hours.
2. Avoid trying to automate all processes at once.
3. Focus on one process to ensure proper implementation and measurable ROI.
