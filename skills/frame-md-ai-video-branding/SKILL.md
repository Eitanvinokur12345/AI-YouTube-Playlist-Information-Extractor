# Frame.md AI Video Branding Workflow

## Overview
Converts a design.md brand specification into a frame.md document that encodes video-specific rules (pacing, scale, motion) for AI agents generating branded video content. Prevents AI from treating 16:9 video frames like web pages.

**Use case:** Teach AI agents to generate on-brand video content by specifying frame-level pacing and motion rules
**Output:** Branded, correctly paced AI-generated video content that matches company motion design guidelines

## Key techniques & how to apply
1. Extend your existing design.md with video-specific sections to create frame.md.
2. Define pacing (cuts per second), scale rules, and motion constraints per frame region.

## General tips
- A frame.md spec file is to video generation what a design system is to UI development — encode the rules once, apply them everywhere.
