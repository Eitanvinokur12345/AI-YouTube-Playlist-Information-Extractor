# Google Maps Screenshot to AI Route Video Workflow

## Overview
Take a screenshot of Google Maps, draw a path on it, and feed it to an AI video generator to produce a realistic first-person route video. The technique lets filmmakers create low-cost drone-style establishing shots without physical filming.

**Use case:** Generating realistic route/flyover videos for short films or content without drone access
**Output:** Realistic AI-generated video following a drawn path over a map screenshot

## Key techniques & how to apply
1. Screenshot Google Maps at the desired location, draw a clear path on the image, then upload to an AI video generator for route video output.
2. This technique is a low-cost alternative to drone footage for establishing shots in short films.
