# Hallucination-Free RAG

## Overview
A technique for Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) that processes source documents (like books) into a structured 'skill' format, allowing AI agents to read specific chapters and provide answers directly from the real content, thereby minimizing or eliminating hallucination.

**Use case:** Ensuring factual accuracy when querying AI models about specific, complex source materials.

## Key steps
1. Utilize tools like 'book-to-skill' to preprocess documents into agent-ready skills.
