# Intelligent Gym

## Overview
Using AI to add 'friction' and challenge your mind, similar to how physical resistance builds muscles, to foster intellectual growth.

**Use case:** Mastering new concepts, deepening understanding, developing critical thinking skills.

## Key steps
1. Study a concept yourself first.
2. Then, use AI as a 'spotter' to quiz you, apply progressive overload (increasing difficulty), and challenge your understanding.
