# Internal Focus Group

## Overview
Lets you run any idea or final product past a group of AI-cloned people who mirror your target audience. Each person becomes their own agent with their voice, lens, and history. Agents run in parallel and return honest, in-character feedback, which is then synthesized into a report.

**Use case:** Getting feedback on ideas or products from a simulated target audience before launch.
