# Iterative Skill Improvement for AI

## Overview
A methodology for continuously refining and enhancing AI skills by updating them based on observed errors, feedback, and new requirements, leading to perpetual improvement.

**Use case:** Ensuring AI agents become more accurate, efficient, and capable over time.

## Key steps
1. Every time the AI messes up, update the corresponding skill.
2. Use feedback loops to identify areas for improvement in skill definitions.
