## Overview
Automatically evaluates incoming leads by gathering company information and classifying them as hot or warm leads to trigger appropriate nurture sequences.

**Use case:** Automates lead scoring and follow-up messaging to assist or replace SDRs in inbound lead management.

## Key steps
1. Integrate with CRM for seamless lead data access.
2. Customize nurture sequences based on lead temperature.
