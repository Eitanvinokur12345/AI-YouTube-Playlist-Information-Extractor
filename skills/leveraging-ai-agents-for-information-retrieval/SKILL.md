# Leveraging AI Agents for Information Retrieval

## Overview
Utilizing AI agents to perform searches and summarize information from large codebases or documents, rather than manually pasting large amounts of data into the AI's context.

**Use case:** Efficiently extracting relevant information from large datasets or codebases without overwhelming the AI's context window.

## Key steps
1. Don't carry the search; let agents do the digging.
2. Your context stays small when agents summarize.
