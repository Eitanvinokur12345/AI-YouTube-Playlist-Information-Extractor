# LLM Council Method

## Overview
A methodology for improving AI decision-making and reducing bias by having multiple AI agents (Contrarian, First Principles, Expansionist, Outsider, Executor) debate a question, peer-review each other's anonymized responses, and then synthesize a final verdict by a Chairman agent.

**Use case:** Obtaining well-rounded, critical, and actionable advice from AI models, especially for complex or sensitive queries, by simulating diverse perspectives and peer review.
