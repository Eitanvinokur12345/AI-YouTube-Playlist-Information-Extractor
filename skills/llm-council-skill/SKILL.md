# LLM Council skill

## Overview
A workflow built on Claude that simulates a council of 5 AI advisors (The Contrarian, First Principles, The Expansionist, The Outsider, The Executor) to provide diverse perspectives on a question. Each advisor attacks the question from a different angle, their responses are peer-reviewed, and a chairman provides a final verdict. This helps overcome the inherent agreeableness of single LLMs.

**Use case:** Decision making, strategic planning, idea validation, problem-solving by getting multi-faceted and critical advice.
