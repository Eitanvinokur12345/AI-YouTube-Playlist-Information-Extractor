# Loop Engineering

## Overview
Designing automated loops that prompt AI agents to perform tasks and achieve goals, rather than directly prompting coding agents.

**Use case:** Automating complex business processes with AI agents.

## Key steps
1. Focus on designing loops that manage agents, not just individual prompts.
2. Utilize worktrees to manage parallel agent tasks and ensure goal alignment.
3. Leverage pre-packaged skills and playbooks for repeatable tasks.
4. Integrate plugins and connectors to interact with external data and services.
5. Employ scalable agent teams and sub-agents for complex workflows.
