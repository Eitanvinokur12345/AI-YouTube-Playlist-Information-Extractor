# Creating a Master Statement for Persistent Context

## Overview
Develop a single comprehensive profile called a master statement that summarizes your learning style, skills, goals, and current projects to provide persistent context to Claude across chats.

**Use case:** Use the master statement to avoid re-explaining yourself in every new chat with Claude, enabling more personalized and relevant responses.

## Key steps
1. Let Claude interview you to generate the master statement automatically.
2. Use the master statement at the start of every new chat to maintain continuity.
3. Keep the master statement concise but comprehensive to cover key personal and professional details.
