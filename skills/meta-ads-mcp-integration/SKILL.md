# Meta Ads MCP Integration

## Overview
Use the official Meta Ads MCP connector to give Claude direct access to your Meta ad account for reporting, budget management, and campaign launching. The connector bridges Claude's reasoning to live ad data, enabling conversational ad management without any manual dashboard work.

## Key Techniques
- Connect Meta Ads MCP to Claude to enable natural language ad account control
- Use Claude to identify underperforming ad sets and pause them instantly
- Shift budgets from losing campaigns to winners through plain English commands

## How to Apply
1. Install the Meta Ads MCP connector in Claude (official Meta connector).
2. Authorize Claude to access your Meta ad account.
3. Use plain English to request reports, pause underperforming ads, adjust budgets, or launch new campaigns.
4. Review results conversationally — no need to open Meta Ads Manager.

## Examples
- "Show me which ad sets are losing money this week and pause them"
- "Shift $500 budget from the underperforming campaign to the winner"
- "Launch a new campaign targeting 25-34 female users in New York"
