# Mitigating AI Hallucination with System Prompts

## Overview
A technique to reduce AI hallucinations by building a multi-part system prompt that instructs the AI to check its own work, be explicit when uncertain, and avoid assuming context.

**Use case:** Preventing AI from generating incorrect or fabricated information (hallucinations) in its responses.

## Key steps
1. Instruct the AI to be explicit when uncertain.
2. Instruct the AI to stop assuming context.
