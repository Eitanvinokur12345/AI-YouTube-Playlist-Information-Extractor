# NAP Consistency Management

## Overview
Ensuring that the business's Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) are identical across all online platforms to help AI consolidate information about the business.

**Use case:** Building a consistent digital footprint for AI verification and improved local SEO.

## Key steps
1. Make your name, address, and phone number identical across every site so AI can join the dots.
