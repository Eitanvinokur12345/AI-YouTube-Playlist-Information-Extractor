# Autonomous AI CMO Workflow with Okara

## Overview
Okara replaces a full marketing team with a coordinated set of autonomous agents running every day: an SEO agent, GEO agent, AI writer for keyword gap content, and community agents that find live conversations on Reddit and Hacker News and draft replies. $99/month vs $14,000+/month for human equivalents.

## Key Techniques
- **SEO agent**: Monitors keyword rankings and surface gaps for content opportunities
- **GEO agent**: Generative Engine Optimization — optimizes content for AI search results
- **AI writer**: Generates articles targeting keyword gaps identified by the SEO agent
- **Reddit/HN agent**: Finds live community discussions relevant to your product and drafts natural replies

## How to Apply
1. Sign up for Okara and connect your website/domain
2. Configure your brand voice, product categories, and target keywords
3. Okara's agent fleet runs daily without intervention:
   - Morning: SEO + GEO agents identify that day's opportunities
   - Midday: AI writer produces articles for the top gaps
   - Continuous: Reddit/HN agents monitor for relevant discussions and draft replies for your review
4. Review and approve community replies before posting
5. Track keyword rank changes and article performance in the Okara dashboard

## Examples
- SaaS startup: Okara publishes 3-5 SEO articles/week on target keywords + engages in 10-15 Reddit threads/week where competitors are mentioned
- Agency: Deploys GEO-optimized content to rank in AI search results (Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT) not just Google
