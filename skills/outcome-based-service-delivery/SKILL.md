# Outcome-Based Service Delivery

## Overview
Shifting from selling one-time automation builds to offering a monthly subscription service based on continuous outcomes delivered by AI employees.

**Use case:** Creating a sustainable, recurring revenue model and aligning incentives with client success.

## Key steps
1. Offer a 90-day outcome guarantee to build initial client confidence.
2. Focus on the value and continuous results the AI employee provides, rather than just the underlying technology.
