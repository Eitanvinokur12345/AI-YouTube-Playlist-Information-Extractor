# Product Sourcing Research

## Overview
Deploying multiple subagents to search various product sourcing websites for popular products, pricing, and order size information.

**Use case:** Market research, competitive analysis, e-commerce product identification.

## Key steps
1. Clearly define the search criteria for popular products.
2. Specify the types of websites to search (e.g., Amazon, other sourcing sites).
3. Instruct subagents on what data points to extract (sourcing, pricing, order size).
