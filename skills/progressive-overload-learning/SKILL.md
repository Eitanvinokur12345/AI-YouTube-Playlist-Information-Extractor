# Progressive Overload (for learning)

## Overview
Applying increasing levels of challenge when learning with AI to strengthen and deepen understanding, similar to building physical muscles.

**Use case:** Structured learning of complex topics, preparing for interviews or challenging situations.

## Key steps
1. Start with basic quizzes (Level 1: high school student).
2. Increase difficulty (Level 2: college student, Level 3: executive interview, Level 4: irate boss challenge).
3. Ask AI to quiz you and provide feedback at each level.
