# Prompt Engineering (Asking Small Questions)

## Overview
Breaking down complex requests into smaller, more specific questions or tasks for the AI to handle one at a time, focusing on individual functions or components rather than entire features.

**Use case:** Getting precise and accurate answers from the AI, preventing it from getting overwhelmed by broad requests.

## Key steps
1. Ask for one function at a time, not the whole feature.
2. Small asks lead to precise answers.
