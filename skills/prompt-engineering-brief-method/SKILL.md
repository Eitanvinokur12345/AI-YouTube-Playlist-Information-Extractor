# Prompt Engineering (Brief Method)

## Overview
A method of interacting with AI models by providing a comprehensive brief upfront, similar to delegating tasks to an engineer, rather than babysitting the AI with iterative, short prompts. This includes defining the goal, constraints, and what 'done' looks like.

**Use case:** Generating content, automating tasks, complex multi-step processes with AI.

## Key steps
1. Treat the AI like an engineer you are delegating to, not a partner you have to babysit.
2. Provide a clear goal, constraints, and definition of 'done' in a single, detailed prompt.
3. Delegate complex tasks in one go and let the AI run autonomously.
