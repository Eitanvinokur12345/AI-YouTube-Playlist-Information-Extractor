# Prompt Engineering for Claude Fable 5

## Overview
Techniques for crafting effective prompts for Claude Fable 5 to achieve optimal results, focusing on brevity, goal-orientation, and boundary setting.

**Use case:** Interacting with Claude Fable 5 for various tasks, ensuring efficient and accurate output.

## Key steps
1. Keep your prompts short.
2. Tell the AI why you want something, not just what you want.
3. Tell the AI exactly what it is not allowed to touch (set boundaries).
4. Lead with the desired outcome in your prompt.
5. Keep output short by being selective about what to include, rather than compressing information into fragments or jargon.
6. Pause for user input only when a task genuinely requires it (e.g., destructive actions, real scope changes, or input only the user can provide).
7. When un-steered, Claude Fable 5 can elaborate beyond what the task needs, especially at higher effort settings; a short brevity instruction is effective.
8. When you have enough information to act, act. Do not re-derive facts already established in the conversation.
9. Do not re-litigate a decision the user has already made.
10. Do not narrate options you will not pursue in user-facing messages.
11. If weighing a choice, give a recommendation, not an exhaustive survey.
