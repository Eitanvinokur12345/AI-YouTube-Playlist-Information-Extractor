# Prompt Engineering for Job Search

## Overview
Crafting detailed and specific prompts for AI models to perform complex tasks like resume analysis, web scraping for job postings, and scoring job fit.

**Use case:** Generating highly relevant job lists, customizing search parameters, ensuring desired output format.

## Key steps
1. Clearly define output format (e.g., table with specific columns).
2. Specify rules for job inclusion (e.g., posted in last 24 hours, minimum fit score).
3. Instruct the AI to be strict with scoring to avoid inflated results.
