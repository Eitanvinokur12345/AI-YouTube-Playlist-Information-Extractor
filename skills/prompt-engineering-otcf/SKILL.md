# Prompt Engineering (Outcome-Task-Context-Format)

## Overview
A structured approach to crafting prompts for AI models by first defining the desired outcome, then the specific task, providing relevant context, and finally specifying the desired output format.

**Use case:** Improving the quality and relevance of AI responses for various tasks, such as lead generation, content creation, and data organization.

## Key steps
1. Start with the ultimate goal (outcome).
2. Define the specific action or request (task).
3. Provide all necessary background information or documents (context).
4. Specify how the output should be presented (format).
