# RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

## Overview
Connects LLMs to external knowledge bases, letting them retrieve relevant facts before generating a response. Reduces hallucinations by grounding responses in verifiable, up-to-date information rather than relying on the model's static training data.

**Use case:** Improving LLM response accuracy and reducing hallucinations by integrating external, up-to-date knowledge.
