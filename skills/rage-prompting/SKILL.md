# Rage prompting

## Overview
A technique where users send angry or exasperated messages to an AI after it has failed them, often involving repeated demands, all caps, insults, or direct commands like 'just do what I asked.'

**Use case:** Understanding and analyzing user frustration in AI interactions; improving AI agent performance by identifying user pain points.
