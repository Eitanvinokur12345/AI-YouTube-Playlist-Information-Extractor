# Reference-Driven Video Creation

## Overview
Instead of starting from a blank prompt, Open Montage can take a reference video (YouTube, Short, Reel, TikTok, or local file) and analyze its transcript, pacing, scenes, keyframes, and style. The agent then builds 2-3 differentiated concept proposals for your new video, grounded in the proven structure and energy of a reference you love.

## Key Techniques
- Paste any video URL as a reference — the agent analyzes what makes it work
- Receive explicit breakdown: what it keeps (pacing, hook style), what it changes (topic, visuals, angle)
- Get cost estimates at your target duration before asset generation starts
- Review a sample before committing to full production

## How to Apply
1. Find a video whose format, pacing, or hook style you want to replicate.
2. Open your Open Montage session in Claude Code or Cursor.
3. Paste the reference URL with your target: `"Here's a Short I love. Make me something like this, but about quantum computing."`
4. The agent analyzes the reference: transcript, scene structure, pacing, visual treatment.
5. You receive 2-3 differentiated concepts with cost estimates and tool paths.
6. Approve a concept; the agent executes the full pipeline from script to rendered video.

## Examples
- "Here's this tech explainer Short. Make me something like this, but about the CRISPR gene editing breakthrough."
- "This cinematic trailer style — I want a 90-second brand film for my SaaS product in the same vein."
- "Take this podcast episode and turn it into a Clip Factory output of 8 ranked social clips, matching the host's pacing."
