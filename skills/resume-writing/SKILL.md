# Resume Writing

## Overview
Crafting professional resumes tailored to specific job descriptions and career goals, incorporating quantifiable achievements and strong action verbs.

**Use case:** Creating optimized resumes for job applicants.

## Key steps
1. Use the XYZ formula (accomplished X as measured by Y by doing Z).
2. Quantify everything possible.
3. Remove fluff language (e.g., 'responsible for', 'assisted with') and replace with strong action verbs.
4. Tailor the entire resume to the specific job description.
5. Match keywords from the job posting naturally without keyword stuffing.
