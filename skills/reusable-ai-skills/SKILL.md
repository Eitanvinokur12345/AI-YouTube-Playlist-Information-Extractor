# Creating Reusable AI Skills

## Overview
The process of converting frequently used or complex prompts into modular, callable skills within an AI environment like Claude Code, allowing for consistent and efficient execution of tasks.

**Use case:** Automating repetitive tasks, standardizing complex workflows, and improving AI's long-term performance.

## Key steps
1. Break down large tasks into smaller, specialized skills (e.g., research, design, testing).
2. Provide comprehensive context including tools, scripts, examples, and relevant files.
3. Continuously update and refine skills based on performance and errors.
