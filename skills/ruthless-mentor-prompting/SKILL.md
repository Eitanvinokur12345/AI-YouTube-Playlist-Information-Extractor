# Ruthless Mentor Prompting

## Overview
A prompting technique that instructs the AI to stress test ideas and provide honest, critical feedback instead of agreeing blindly.

**Use case:** Use this prompt to get AI to challenge your ideas and improve their quality by pointing out flaws and weaknesses.

## Key steps
1. Insert the exact line: 'Be my ruthless mentor. You have to stress test everything. If my ideas are trash, tell me why and get things to the point where they're bulletproof.'
2. Use in AI settings or prompt start to set tone for conversation.
