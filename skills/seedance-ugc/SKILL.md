# Seedance 2.0 UGC Workflow

## Overview
A workflow that pairs the Seedance 2.0 video model (accessed via Arcads) with Claude to clone competitor UGC ads at scale. Claude analyzes a winning user-generated ad, then Seedance regenerates similar videos with AI actors, replacing expensive creator shoots.

**Use case:** Mass-produce UGC-style video ads without hiring creators.
**Output:** AI-generated UGC video ads.

## Key techniques & how to apply
1. Have Claude analyze a winning UGC ad before regenerating it.
2. Use Arcads to access Seedance 2.0.
3. Swap AI actors to A/B test creatives cheaply.
