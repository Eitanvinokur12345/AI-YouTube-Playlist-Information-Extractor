# Skill Entropy reward

## Overview
A training signal that quantifies the difficulty of switching between reasoning skills, used to optimize models for multi-skill reasoning and improve accuracy on cross-skill tasks.

**Use case:** Boosting multi-skill reasoning in AI models; improving accuracy on cross-skill tasks by turning a difficulty metric into a training signal.
