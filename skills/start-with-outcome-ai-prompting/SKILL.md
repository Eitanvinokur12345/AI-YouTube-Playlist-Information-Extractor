# Start with the outcome in AI prompting

## Overview
Before instructing AI on 'what to do,' clearly articulate the desired outcome or 'why' the task is being performed. Providing the AI with the specific goal enhances its understanding and leads to more accurate and relevant responses.

**Use case:** Improving the quality and relevance of AI-generated content or solutions.

## Key steps
1. Always tell the AI the specific goal or outcome you are trying to achieve.
