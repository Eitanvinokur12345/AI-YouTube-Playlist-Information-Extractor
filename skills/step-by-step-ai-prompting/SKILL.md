# Step-by-step AI Prompting

## Overview
A technique to get better results from AI by breaking down a complex task into multiple, sequential prompts, where each step builds upon the output of the previous one. This avoids messy answers from single, large prompts.

**Use case:** Content creation (ideas, hooks, scripts), email generation, planning, decision support, general problem-solving with AI.

## Key steps
1. Start with broad idea generation.
2. Select the best option from the initial output.
3. Refine and dive deeper into the selected option with subsequent prompts.
