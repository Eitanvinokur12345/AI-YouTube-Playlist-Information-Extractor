# The Council

## Overview
A method for using Claude involving five specialized AI agents (The Contrarian, First Principles Thinker, The Expansionist, The Outsider, The Executor) to analyze a problem from different perspectives, followed by peer review and a final verdict from a chairman agent, to reduce AI 'lying' or hallucination.

**Use case:** Problem-solving, critical analysis, decision-making, reducing AI bias/hallucination.
