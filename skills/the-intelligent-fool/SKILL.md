# The Intelligent Fool

## Overview
Embracing a 'beginner's mind' and having the courage to admit what you don't know, using AI to fill knowledge gaps without ego.

**Use case:** Continuous learning, overcoming intellectual arrogance, fostering neuroplasticity.

## Key steps
1. Identify areas where you lack understanding, even if others expect you to know.
2. Ask AI the most basic questions, simplifying explanations multiple times.
3. Embrace feeling 'stupid' as a sign of learning and growth.
