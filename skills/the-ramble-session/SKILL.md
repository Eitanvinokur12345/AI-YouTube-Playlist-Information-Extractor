# The Ramble Session

## Overview
A technique for providing context to LLMs or AI agents by speaking freely and stream-of-consciousness in voice mode for an extended period, aiming for a 'total mess' of thoughts rather than structured prompts.

**Use case:** Generating rich, unstructured context for AI agents to improve their understanding and performance.
