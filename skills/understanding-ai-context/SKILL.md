# Understanding AI Context

## Overview
The ability to comprehend how AI models maintain conversational context, including the concept of a 'context window' and its limitations, to better interact with and prompt AI.

**Use case:** Crafting more effective prompts, managing long conversations with AI, troubleshooting AI 'forgetfulness'.

## Key steps
1. Keep conversations focused to avoid exceeding the context window.
2. Reiterate key information if a conversation becomes very long.
3. Understand that AI doesn't have persistent memory across different chat sessions.
