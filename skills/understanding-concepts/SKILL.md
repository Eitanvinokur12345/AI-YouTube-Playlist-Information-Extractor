# Understanding Concepts

## Overview
Focusing on the underlying principles and ideas behind tools and frameworks, rather than just memorizing syntax or specific implementations. This allows for quicker adaptation to new technologies and updates.

**Use case:** Adapting to new AI frameworks and updates, becoming a more versatile engineer.

## Key steps
1. Don't just memorize the syntax of a tool; understand the concepts it implements.
2. Focus on the 'why' and 'how' of a technology, not just the 'what'.
