# Using Direct Examples as AI Inputs

## Overview
Instead of describing what you want, provide AI with direct examples such as screenshots, files, or real references to improve output relevance.

**Use case:** Enhance AI responses by feeding it concrete examples rather than vague instructions.

## Key steps
1. Attach or embed real examples when prompting AI.
2. Avoid vague or abstract instructions.
