# Video Analysis via AI (Frame-by-Frame)

## Overview
The technique of breaking down a video into individual still frames (one per second) and a transcript, then feeding these to an AI model (like Claude) to analyze the visual and textual content, effectively allowing the AI to 'read' the video like a flipbook.

**Use case:** Detailed video content analysis, summarization, scene change detection, shot list generation, understanding visual context alongside spoken words.

## Key steps
1. Use YT-DLP to download the video.
2. Use FFmpeg to extract frames (e.g., one frame per second) and generate a transcript.
3. Upload the extracted frames and transcript to Claude for analysis.
