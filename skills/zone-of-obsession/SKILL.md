# Zone of Obsession

## Overview
Focusing human effort on tasks with uncapped payoffs, where small improvements can lead to disproportionately large results.

**Use case:** Strategic planning, product design, customer interaction, complex problem-solving.

## Key steps
1. Identify tasks with uncapped payoffs (e.g., product design, pricing models).
2. Pour significant human effort and creativity into these tasks, as AI cannot fully replace human judgment and intuition here.
